# Gone Poof

Why does it feel so permanent? I don't understand. How can this whole thing collapse like a house of cards. It was ~~huge & imposing~~ ~~[?]~~ wonderful & ephemeral, and ~~[?]~~ now gone poof.
Camera Obscura - difference in light - two spaces of different light intensity.
`[sketch: a flat platform on a post with a smaller box set into it]`
`[sketch: a box frame in perspective on a slab]`
`[sketch: a box seen from the side, with lamps shining in from the left ("bright"), a dark space above ("dark"), and labels "square of lights" and "dark - mirrored world."]`
`[an arrow runs from "now gone poof" down to:]` It's so rediculous. The distance just allows it to vanish. There wasn't really anything there except a happiness behind - a glow
