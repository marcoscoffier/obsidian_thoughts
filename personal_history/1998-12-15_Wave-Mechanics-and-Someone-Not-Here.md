# Wave Mechanics, and Someone Not Here

e⁻ - can be in a variety of different places, at once (described by a wave function) until you measure it → then it collapses to one place. Like anything really, when someone is not here you can imagine them in a variety of places depending on what you know about them - until you find them and this variety collapses down to one physical location.
<u>Wave mechanics</u>

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated. e⁻ is an electron.
