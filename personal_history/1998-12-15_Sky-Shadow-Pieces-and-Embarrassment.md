# Sky Shadow Pieces, and Embarrassment

I think of the Sky shadow pieces and the intense embarassement I felt when setting them up - but also sureness of what I was doing. All of the workmanship imitating an industrial production in fact is an attempt to make the special moment I am framing seem absolutely normal. There is beauty in ~~that~~ normalcy. In the sky shadow piece: building the frame was a way of protecting this simple idea - protecting it from those who would laugh it off. this protection is somewhat tennuous for it may be even stupider to say look at that guy who spent so long building that thing - but that time validates an action `[an arrow from "spent", written up the margin:]` gives it valeur

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated. "Sky Shadow" (also "Sun Shadow") and "Sky Color" were outdoor pieces from Marco's last year at Yale (1995).
