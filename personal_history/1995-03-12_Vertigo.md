# Vertigo

Vertigo = thought you can feel running up behind you, ready to tackle you into the void.
It is almost hard to keep oneself back from jumping off a cliff or infront of an oncoming subway train. I always thought that the word "vertigo" included this attraction but every dictionary & encyclopedia entry I have found can't seem to get by passing vertigo off as a dizzyness or queasy feeling ~~[?]~~ associated w/ motion or heights. This is not what I have ever thought vertigo meant. I imagine that reading criticism on Hitchcock's movie would fill in some gaps between the dictionary definition and the ~~word~~ meaning I am looking for.
When the narrator in <u>Terre des hommes</u> lies on his back on the top of an isolated plateau in the desert, he feels a vertiginous attraction to the stars. he feels ~~a~~ ~~[?]~~ force pulling up wards or off the planet into the universe
