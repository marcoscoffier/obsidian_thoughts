> [!note] Transcription notes
> The inside front cover. The New Haven return address is crossed out and replaced by the Washington DC one, so the DC address was probably added later, after Yale. The New Haven zip code reads "06570" or "06520" (Yale's zip code is 06520). Shozawa is Katherine's last name, so the Vancouver address is her family's. Turrell is James Turrell, whose Roden Crater studio was in Flagstaff; Dan Graham and Robert Irwin are artists. "Jauchum + Meeh" is the Brooklyn special-effects firm Jauchem & Meeh. The French quotation's source ("p. 38") isn't given. Undated: the notebook's first entry is 12/28/94.
