# García Márquez on Poets

There is no one with more common sense, no stonecutter more obstinate, no manger more lucid or dangerous than a poet.
Gabriel Garcia Marquez <u>Love in the time of cholera</u>.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated, in blue ink; between 7/25 and 8/20/95. "manger" is as written; the published translation reads "no manager more lucid".
