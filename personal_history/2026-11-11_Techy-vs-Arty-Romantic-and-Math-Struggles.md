# Techy vs. Arty Romantic & Family Education Resentment

Stereotypes: Hurtful Venn diagram on SNL. Jokes about spies being into tech guys. The drop-off of intimacy with Mi coincides with our return to the US and me taking up tech roles. Why the public allergy to STEM people? I feel this allergy very strongly with my susceptible son who refuses the pursuit of tech/engineering/arch despite being well adapted to this work. I mentioned how tacky I found Jensen’s house. I can only appreciate a part of each of my overlapping worlds.

I sort of survived, by fighting and re-organizing my life. Off to a late start, constant catch-up and workarounds. My brothers fared much worse. The albatross of Papa's Polytechnique lesson books. Anger that I didn’t have the support to push and do the hard things.
