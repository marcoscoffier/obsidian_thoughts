# Pocahontas, the Perfect Crush

Pocahantas
The perfect crush
Ethnic and athletic
a tom-boy woman
out for her own
in nature
perfectly attractive in her unattainableness
The bright colored computer generated
character opens your heart your imagination
~~You have~~ Yet the image remains of the fat animator
at his computer choosing the perfect
colors and calculating the cuteness
factor in large eyes or a head that
is too big for her body, but
for some reason when you are
off your guard, she gets you
like she gets millions like you
somehow Disney knows something
human they've converted it into pixels and charts
but it works, cause they've targeted
your soft-spot and you don't have
enough defense to ward off all
~~the onslaughts of commercialism~~
~~pointed~~ ~~towards~~ ~~mass~~ ~~mentality~~
of commercialism's pointed onslaughts
~~to create mass mentality~~
and you are caught in the flow
of mass mentality
`[right margin, written at an angle, with an arrow from "somehow Disney knows something human":]` The same human something that is responcible for the increased sale of white Ford Bronco's after the OJ trial or that human something Beethoven was after? can they be separated?
