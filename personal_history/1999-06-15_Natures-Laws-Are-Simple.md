# Nature's Laws Are Simple

Natures essential laws are simple b/c people chose the simplest explanations to describe something - sometimes different description systems result in simpler descriptions → spherical vs. polar etc coordinates - people could chose more complicated reasons which explain equally well - but don't thus nature seems simple.
As a detective is likely to chose the simplest explanation for a few facts. A criminal is protected by a complicated crime.
