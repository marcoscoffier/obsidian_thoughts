# Places Remembered: Houston, Écoles, Skye

`[bracketed:]`
\* New York - large emptiness @ west end of Houston? near Jen Rensy's
→ Paris - Ave des écoles vers St. Michel near Jussieu
→ Washington - Connecticut ave near Uptown theater.
New Haven only real memories are the roof tops.
