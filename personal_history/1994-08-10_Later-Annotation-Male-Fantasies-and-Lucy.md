# Later Annotation: Male Fantasies & Lucy vs. Katherine

*(Later annotation: I hate this page and want to tear it out. I have not given Sarah—or anybody who would fall into that category for me—a thought in quite a long time. I think of those fantasies I had as painful to a relationship I really care about, the way I would like to care about a relationship. It is about people and not genitalia. I find these male fantasies as painful as I found my singing group congratulating me for having gotten together with Lucy, when they never said anything about Katherine. I guess Katherine was more invisible to them because our relationship was so private.)*
