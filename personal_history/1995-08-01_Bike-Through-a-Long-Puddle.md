# Bike Through a Long Puddle

I wizz over a long puddle on my bike early in the morning.
Early in the morning, ~~I~~ my bike ~~zips~~ through a long puddle
`[boxed:]`
Early in the morning
I wizz over a long puddle
on my bike.
`[to the right:]`
Before the low sun
My bike ~~rips~~ w[hips] through a
long puddle -
`[crossed through with zigzags:]` ~~Sunny this morning~~
~~We wizz over a long puddle~~
~~my bike and I.~~
`[in pencil:]`
Early in the morning
my bike wizzes through
a long puddle.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated; drafts of a short poem, each version a rewrite of the last. The correction in "My bike ... through" is a "w" written over "rips", probably making "whips"; that reading is a guess.
