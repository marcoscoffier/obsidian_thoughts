# A New Essentialism

1/17 I think that I will come up w/ a new type of essentialism, "convert them or fuck them" - who cares. If they don't see it, then that's their ~~[?]~~ tough. In the fine line of Diebenkorn, Turrell.
I write this on the metal steps of Hammond Hall where the sun has just pierced a cloudy sky making 2 telephone wires very red, a van electric green and a couple houses wonderfully bright against the dark sky.
I accept exceptions. I just don't concern myself w/ them.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> A reply to the previous day's lecture on essentialism, [[1995-01-16_Art-Major-Meeting-Essentialism]]. Hammond Hall is a Yale building on Mansfield Street.
