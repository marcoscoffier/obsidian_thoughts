# What We Learn Isn't What We're Taught

What we learn isn't what we're taught nor what we study.
<u>We</u> <u>don't know</u> <u>what</u> <u>we're</u> <u>learning</u>
\- A teacher should do ~~more~~ something other than fill in the gaps
~~what we learn isn't what~~
↳ Cage.

"synchronicity" - btw. dance & music
independant but in the same time.

Bollingen, Wilhelm/Baynes edition w/ essay on synch. by Jung.
John Dewey <u>Art as experience</u>
anarchic harmony   decentralized

> [!note] Transcription notes
> The synchronicity of dance and music is Cage and Cunningham's way of working. The Bollingen edition is the Wilhelm/Baynes translation of the *I Ching*, which has Jung's foreword (Jung's essay on synchronicity is separate).
