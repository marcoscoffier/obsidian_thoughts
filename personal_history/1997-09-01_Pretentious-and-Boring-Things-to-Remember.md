# Pretentious and Boring; Things to Remember

pretentious and boring is the worst possible combination of personality traits and ever present at the Beaux-Arts
[l'Art pour l'art mentioned by Marie (of course) at Bustamente's openning as in the Japanese don't have concept of l'art pour l'art - they are so connected to craftsmanship (Busta.) was about the limit of what I could handle in that one of the worst most pretentiously boring shows I have ever visited.
