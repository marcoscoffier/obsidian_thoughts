# Analytical Color & Angle Diagram: Edward Hopper’s *Rooms by the Sea* (1951)

* **Artwork Studied:** Edward Hopper, *"Rooms by the Sea,"* 1951 (Yale University Art Gallery).
* **Diagram Annotations (Left to Right):**
  * **Upper Left & Doorway:** `"can see ceiling line"`, `"glazes"`, `"sharper angle"`, `"blue green"`, `"diff angle"`, `"red"` (light/dark split on jamb), `"almost horizontal"`.
  * **Central Sunlit Wall & Floor:** `"same & same height"`, `"blue grey w/ purple"` (diagonal shadow), `"rather thick"`, `"pink"`, `"almost pure white"`, `"blue"`, `"yellow"`, `"grn/yl/brwn"`, `"horizontal →"`.
  * **Right Doorway & Comparison:** `"warm green blue"`, `"warm blue"`, `"purple shadow dark"`, `"cer. cobalt"`, `"green"`, `"whitest"`, `"white waves thickest paint pale pthalo"` → `"not thick like Childe Hassam's 'The West Wind, Isles of Shoals' 1904"`.
* **Bottom Synthesis Notes:**
  * `- does operate tonally`
  * `- strong feeling of sunlight`
  * `- color game: if there is pure white, it is painted on top of other colors & does not appear so except in top corner of sunlight near top of the door.`
