# Belonging & Secret Tricks

You asked if I felt I belonged. That is a really hard question for me. I honestly don’t feel I belong anywhere. I like having a secret trick up my sleeve—bike messenger taking showers in the Justice Dept., Hawley Waterman comment, driving a robot in Sandy Hook, DARPA demo with Clement. When I see the videos and pictures of my dad setting up antennas in Iran with his family and friends thousands of miles away, it really resonates.
