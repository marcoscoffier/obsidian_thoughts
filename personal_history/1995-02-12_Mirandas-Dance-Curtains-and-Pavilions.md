# Miranda's Dance: Curtains and Pavilions

<u>Miranda's dance</u>: I must find out where we are going to do this
\- I must go to the <u>theatre store</u> - black paint & black curtain
\- white spray paint - black fire resistant tape for halogen
\- look @ lights.
Already the priorities are getting confused, they are all a priority
The problem w/ all these proposals trying to get space is that they are all so concerned w/ the hardware - that is not what the art is about, I can't believe I spent time making the hook loop today which has so little to do w/ the piece. The plate and hook are to fasten the wire which holds the wall which captures the light - at least two removes from the light
\* could we get ~~[?]~~ dancing Tech people to hold the curtains up on long poles - the space is organic dancing!
`[sketch: two running figures holding up a striped curtain on poles]`
`[sketch: two poles meeting at an angle, labelled "what is this joint"]`
`[sketch: a rectangular frame braced with crossed cables, with a weight hanging at one side]` - collapseable - too hard to do in such a short time, but wow. space openning & collapsing w/ the grace & swiftness of a bird's wing - like one of Renzo Piano's pavillions.
`[sketch: a long box labelled "Aluminum pipes", with arrows]` rubber stoppers on the feet - tension thing w/ cables
`[sketch: a curtain hung between two pipes, labelled "articulation", "2 pipes", "cable", "how to make this stable" and "on wheels!"]`
`[sketch: a cubic frame of pipes braced with cables, labelled "cables?"]` - <u>super light</u> - collapsable easy to put together screws & bolts
`[in a box, written at an angle:]` <u>Tomorrow</u> `[boxed:]` Cementitious[?] - Miranda & I have to play w/ halogen & scrims in the room
