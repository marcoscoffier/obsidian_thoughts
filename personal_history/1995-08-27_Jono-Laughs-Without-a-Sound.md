# Jono Laughs Without a Sound

8/27 I love when Jono laughs so hard that he makes no sound; he rocks way backwards or forwards in his chair opens his mouth wide & his eyes squint up but he makes no sound at all. I was very happy to go to the Brickskellar last night w/ Dave, Jono & Schwartz - re telling stories from high-school asking a waitress for a beer we used to get, which they haven't had since she ~~was~~ has been working there - which we used to get there when we were 16.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> The Brickskeller was a beer bar in Washington DC; Jono is a friend from high school; Dave and Schwartz seem to be high-school friends too.
