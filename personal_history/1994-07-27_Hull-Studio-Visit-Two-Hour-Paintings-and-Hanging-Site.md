# Hull's Studio Visit, 2-Hour Paintings & Hanging 3D Site Idea (7/27)

**7/27** *got a new pen @ Charette!*

Hull came to see my paintings today. I was a little disappointed. He still likes a long skinny one I did from the model in 50 mins. He mentioned seeing the artist's excitement in the paint. I haven't been able to convert [above: *convey*] my excitement of the landscape into paintings that are exciting. He suggested & I'm definitely going to do it, limiting my time say to 2 hrs. & knowing I am going to make one 2hr painting a day for 2 weeks - or the whole time I'm in France. I hope being w/ my family won't curb my activity, but I'm sure it will.

> *(Left margin annotation, arrow to "2 hrs."):* `(I forgot about this & made paintings that were too long in Lytle's class.)`

He mentioned Frank Auerbach who repaints the whole image every day he approaches it again.

I had a vision while Hull was talking to me of making a huge free painting then on another day going in w/ life & excitement to get one part of the painting much more carefully detailed. Something would have to be going on between the free & the detailed so that the image wouldn't fall apart. Also, I thought of all the paintings I have seen within the larger landscapes I did or all the paintings that can be found in a particular sight [above: *site*] `[staple canvas to big board. For the smaller paintings, make them on new pieces of canvas stapled on]`.

I could recreate that site w/ multiple still images until I had created a 3 dimensional view of it. I could hang the boards on fishing wire in appropriate spots to create a space (of different snapshots, w/ my different moods & the changing weather) over a specific period of days. That way the many quick images which could quickly become junk, could hold together in some larger whole.

2-sided boards could most easily be viewed hanging from the ceiling. I like this better than on a pedestal like a sculpture.
