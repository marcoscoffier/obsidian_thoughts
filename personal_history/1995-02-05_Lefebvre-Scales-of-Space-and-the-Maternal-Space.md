# Lefebvre, Scales of Space and the Maternal Space

2/5 The ~~production~~ of Space Lefebvre
"From... a philosophy of space revised and corrected by mathematics - the modern field of inquiry known as epistemology has inherited and adopted the notion that the status of space is that of a 'mental thing' or 'mental place'." p.3
That space is in the mind explains why / how one can switch through scales so easily. I can imagine down into the atoms which comprise the ink flowing out of the tip of my pen. I can imagine the distances from the electron clouds to the nucleus. I can also think outward - think of the tiny spek I become on the surface of the planet and how the planet itself becomes a tiny spec in the universe, not even 1/100,000th of the sun which is only a medium sized star. Science has opened our mental paths to these radical oppositions because it has managed to label & to quantify these distances, these spaces, these differences. But I doubt one needed scientific proof of the atom to experience the vertiginous conceptualization of its existence. → see Lucretius
