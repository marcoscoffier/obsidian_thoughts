# Asking the Viewer to Do Things; a Tower

I am thinking of asking my viewer to do things - to copy a poem because that forces one to read it in a different way everyone reads all reading becomes skimming -
Artist = teacher - like Wordsworth says in the Prelude
could I make them get naked some where where they would never be seen, a place to experience your own envolvement w/ nature. - <u>Emerson</u>
They will click the switch - in my sculpture room.
My hiking or biking movie that I thought of this past summer is tempting me extraordinarily - as is getting a video camera for graduation.
\- I could show the movie in a box like those sit down video games or a passport photo booth - (o the day when I could pay someone to build me these things)
I want to make a tower some where where there are no high buildings. I could even put blinders on the tower so that no one could see who ever was up there unless they were a mile away or more. But the viewer would have a view of the horizon. Like being on top of a mountain. The viewer could get completely naked no one would see - but they would be there facing the world `[a small sketch of the blinders, two slanted strokes, beside "were a mile away"]`
