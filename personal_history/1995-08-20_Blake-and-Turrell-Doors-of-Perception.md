# Blake and Turrell: Doors of Perception

Blake: If the doors to perception where ~~closed~~ cleansed, everything would appear to man as it is, infinite.
Turrell is a very powerful artist - I realise this again reading about his interaction w/ Irwin & Wortz -
The US is so literal he says: has to go to moon to see infinite can't see it in a rock like the Japanese - a regular 'Japanophile (?)
\_\_\_
\- get a 2nd hand book on Astronomy, Navigation, Meinels, ECKrupp, Turrell, DuChamp.
