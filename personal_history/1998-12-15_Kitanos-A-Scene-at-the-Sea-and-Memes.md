# Kitano's "A Scene at the Sea", and Memes

I had a thought the other day watching kitano's "a day at the sea". the beginning's build up to his buying a surf board was quite nice. at the very beginning he is collecting trash and stops to look at the sea. we don't know that he is deaf yet but he's in another world. What blocks us from looking at the sea when we want? Many people (two men) would feel uncomfortable in that situation → "aw - let's go to the bar, there's no point in standing

here like idiots". What is the impulse that makes people act in a certain way, that you have to be in another world, deaf, etc. not to feel moved `[below:]` & impelled. by these cultural exigencies -
These forces are <u>memes</u> I think.
