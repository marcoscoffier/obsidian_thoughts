## [[2005-03-21_New-Start-the-Dialog-as-a-Learning-Process|New Start: the Dialog as a Learning Process]]
*2005-03-21 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1997_2_p084.jpg|p. 84]]*

`[a blue sticky tab at the top right:]` New start. Mar 21 2005.
\- a dialog is actually a brilliant way of structuring a learning process. In pre-literate socratic times this would be obvious. but today w/ book based literacy so fundamental to much of the educative process the power of the dialog is not so obvious.
\_\_\_
The other strength of the dialog is that not everything must be expressed some `[above:]` can be simply implied, perhaps the two characters have understood but has the audience?
\_\_\_
Problem is really that the reader of a dialog is a 3rd person who has no place really. A fly on the wall a hopeless outsider.
\_\_\_
dialog project - each person gets only their part to read a kind of karaoke - lights up when it is your turn to read - [cell phone radio play.]
\_\_\_
`[bracketed:]` People talk at the same time - emptiness btw. words - meaning btw the cracks. Dialog btw all my journal notes? → Text-to speech? ← cell phone - blog -
