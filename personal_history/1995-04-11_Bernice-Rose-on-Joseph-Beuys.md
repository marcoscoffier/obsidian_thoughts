# Bernice Rose on Joseph Beuys

<u>Bernise Rose</u> - Joseph Beuys `[above:]` b. 1921 12 in 1933 - Hitler youth groups
felt & fat to save his life by Tartars in Crimea.
1951 - started making sculpture w/ Materay labeled a degenerate artist in Hitler's time - anything that wasn't social realism
1955-6 - mental break down. worked in fields. 56-7
Thousands of drawings 47 - 61 → prof. @ Dusseldorf Acad.
\- involved w/ fluxus. new work of John Cage.
d. 1986.
time @ Acad. - extremely politicized stud. rebellion spread from France.
social sculpture - everyone is an artist - everyone participant in formation of new society - act of making society = a concrete structure.
<u>drawings</u> -
Beuys applies his theories as antidotes to Hitler supported mad-scientists.
creates myth of re-birth for himself → picked up in Crimea.
Romantic - details, particulars `[stacked:]` macro / micro seen in
\- fragments, idealisation of part
each of his drawings is a fragment - float in space.
spiritual place in creative soup.
Materay - crystaline structures under all matter.
\- reflection of secret order of the universe.
\- man is the measure - Leonardo.
Picasso - cubism through analysis of human figure.
"Drawing, 1st physical manifestation of the artist's thought"
~~Leonardo~~ Leonardo - artist has power greater than nature he should invent.
Beuys chaotic energy - mind etc. through circulation warmed & finds order then becomes cold & solid matter.
Beuys alchemist - material - Iodine.
substance has metaphysical meaning.
Stiener - bees ideal social community → Bueys: wax
Cages idea of notation - sound in a field longer sound = longer notation.

Aesthetization of every aspect of life - under Hitler back to Rousseau and many others in Anglo-Fr. Germ. tradition.
aesthetic - social force not just in art.
In Fluxus performance EurAsia - Utopia now ~~[?]~~ place
begins to make assemblages.
forces of the performance.
drawing to explain - these are the later drawings.
everything to be reconciled.
Intuition highest overall.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated. Notes from a talk by Bernice Rose, the MoMA drawings curator, on Joseph Beuys's drawings. "Materay" is Ewald Mataré, Beuys's teacher at Düsseldorf (and himself labelled "degenerate" under the Nazis). "Stiener" is Rudolf Steiner, whose lectures on bees influenced Beuys. "Iodine" is a reading; Beuys's materials were usually felt, fat, wax and honey.
