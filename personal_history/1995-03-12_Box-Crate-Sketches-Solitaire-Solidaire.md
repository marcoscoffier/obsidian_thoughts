> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated; a page of sketches between the 3/12 entries and 3/14. "Naumann's space under my chair" is Bruce Nauman's *A Cast of the Space Under My Chair* (1965–68). "Camus' painter" is Jonas, in "Jonas ou l'artiste au travail" from *L'Exil et le royaume*, whose last word, written on his canvas, is either "solitaire" or "solidaire". "lettre a amie inventée" is Saint-Exupéry's *Lettres à l'amie inventée*. "warm", "q floods" and "dbl." are uncertain readings.
