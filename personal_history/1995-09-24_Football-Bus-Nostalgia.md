# Football Bus Nostalgia

9/24 - I had a strangely wonderful feeling of nostalgia this morning when I loaded onto the bus carrying Richie ~~and~~, Coach Markoff and the football team to their game at a small private school 2hrs. away. The smell of the grass and the pads. The big green bus.
Also, being in D.C. in the fall, something which I know, but haven't experienced for the last 4 years. Its like hearing the voice of an old friend for the 1st time in a long time.
The changing of the seasons always makes me nostalgic.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Autumn 1995, back in Washington DC after the four Yale years. Richie is Marco's brother, so this is presumably his football team; Coach Markoff is not identified.
