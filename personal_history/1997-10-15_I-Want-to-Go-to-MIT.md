# I Want to Go to MIT

I want to go to <u>MIT</u> to take classes in Cognitive Science & Linguistics - in Electronics & Computer Programming
↳ the classes for Physicists where they can learn to build test apparatus.
Public Art has always been interesting - but my main ambition is to take everything I can learn from MIT to really jump start my science & technology knowledge and capabilities.
The intervention in the city will probably be very light - travel & leave no traces, Tony Hiss whisper arch, or times square beauty - re-discovery
↳ <u>Letter to Ron Jones</u> ←
