# Painting vs. Camera, Self-Disclosure & Thinking of Marrying Katherine

Today, taking pictures, I thought it's silly for the painter to fight to represent exactly what a camera sees. A painting is a painting—everyone knows this, so I might as well enjoy it for what it is.

The first 28 pages of this book (not including the notes I have since added) took me a year to write, and I have almost finished the rest in two months. Somehow I am glad that I wrote down this beginning—I write down too much. Maybe it would be more mysterious if I hid these things, like I think I should hide some things in conversation, and not make myself so crystal clear.

*(Later annotation: I still feel these things, probably even stronger—if that is possible, because they weren't feelings that had to do with strength or measurement of any kind; they are good and solid. I've been thinking about living with Katherine—marrying?—a lot lately.)*
