# Toilet Paper, Mirrors and the Platonic Theory of Sight

1/22
`[sketch: two long strips hanging side by side, each with a fold near the bottom]`
\- two sheets of T.P. hanging in a black room, "looking" at eachother. the viewer walks in w/ a flash light. When he shines the light between them they reflect eachother infinitely very effemeral moment.
`[sketch: two rounded half-shells facing each other, the left one with a window and a candle inside, an arrow running from one to the other]`
Platonic theory of sight. Rays from inside your head `[sketch: two cones point to point, joined by a dotted line with arrows]` bounce & return the information.
That is why it is so special to catch someone else's eye & so arresting to look at oneself in a mirror.
`[sketch: two half-shells facing each other, each with a head in profile looking in through its curved back]`
`[sketch: a small flashlight]` - makes the infinite possible - what does this exterior light mean.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> "T.P." is toilet paper. The sketches seem to show a viewing room for two, like [[1995-01-11_Room-for-Two-Viewers]].
