# Chinese Painting at the Yale University Art Gallery (YUAG)

Chinese painting at YUAG: One is very aware that this is about the distinctness and limitation of brush on paper, yet there is a deep feeling for nature and the seeing of it. The mist at the bottom of the mountains is simply the blank paper. One can tell the different brushes or strokes that were used to evoke the landscape, and there is also a deep sense of the human—small—within the landscape.
