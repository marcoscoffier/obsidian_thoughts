# Jumbled Thoughts & Objectifying the Therapist

ADHD notebook. Always rambling.

Jumbled thoughts: Sometimes I feel guilty that I toot my own horn in these sessions. I’m searching for confidence—who am I? I feel invisible to so many worlds. I dream of total connection. I discount people for lacking one aspect or another—I’m afraid to ask questions about you lest I be disappointed. A. had homely clothes and a simplistic taste in music. I am really an ass. Mi and I seemed to align on so much, and I had just read *Nora* about Joyce’s uneducated wife/muse.

I’ve tried to keep you in the place of a caricature: the sleek, knowledgeable therapist who effortlessly places her clients into the correct box for their character weaknesses and prescribes the actions to set their lives on a path to power and success and happiness. I objectify you in this role. I hear you probing for more human connection. I find this destabilizing. What happens if I know the human inside this caricature? I can be let down: you are too simple, you will never understand me.

*(Fragments: Nora – Joyce’s muse; Wittgenstein and Heidegger in their nature getaways.)*
