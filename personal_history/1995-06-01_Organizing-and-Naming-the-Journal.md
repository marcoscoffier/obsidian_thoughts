# Raw Thoughts: Organizing & Naming the Journal

I think that it makes sense to try to organize these thoughts, at least try to group them, like I have been doing with images. I become more active in their interaction and can recognize interconnections from which further thoughts can grow. Once I pull them from the sea in which they now lie, they can come to have greater meaning—it will be like taking this whole system a step further.

First, I have been recording the thoughts (naming them, so they took a shape and identity on the page); now I am going to name them again by categorizing them. 

The confidence I am gaining from this book is incredible—there are already the beginnings of enough thoughts to last me a lifetime. May I never lose it!
