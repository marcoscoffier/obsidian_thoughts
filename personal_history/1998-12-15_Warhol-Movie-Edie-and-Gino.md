# Warhol Movie: Edie and Gino

I watched a warhol movie yesterday - precursor to web-cams™, but far more interesting to watch. He was endlessly talking to `[in red:]` a lying, almost naked diagonally across the screen Edie, who was lying in bed w/ Gino who was brought into this situation by warhol for Edie's pleasure, he says. but she doesn't really disagree. It was like a live Beckett play. Edie like us is mesmerized by warhol's weird rambling voice. He always manages to say something that catches her attention, breaks her succumbing to Gino's advances. Gino like his prodding hands only want's to have sex but Edie can't turn off Warhol
The dumb sexual desire of Gino is needed to catalyze a situation
\- <u>Radical</u> -
