# Roof-Top Mirror Floor

<u>Roof-top</u>: <u>mirror floor</u>. <u>blank</u> <u>walls</u> which block your view of the city and that ~~[?]~~ capture the changing light.

<u>grid</u> - somehow mapping your perception of space making a ~~[?]~~ unit of measure
