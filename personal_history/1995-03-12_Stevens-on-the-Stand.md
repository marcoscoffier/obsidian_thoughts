# Stevens on the Stand

I want to put Steven's' poem "An Ordinary Evening in New Haven" ch. IX on my stand on the roof of the A&A. I want to put two scrims on buildings where the sun rises to catch its light & glow.
`[sketch: two lines meeting at an angle, like a view from a point]` perspective - I can get you to see what I want you to see if I know where you are going to stand.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated. Section IX of Wallace Stevens's "An Ordinary Evening in New Haven" (1950) begins "We keep coming back and coming back / To the real".
