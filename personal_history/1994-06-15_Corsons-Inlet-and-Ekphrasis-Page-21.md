# A.R. Ammons’s *“Corson’s Inlet”* & Ekphrasis Margin Notes (Page 21)

> **Corson's Inlet:**  
> I was released from forms,  
> from the perpendiculars  
> straight lines, blocks, boxes, binds  
> of thought  
> into the hues, shadings, rises, flowing bends and blends  
> of **sight** *(circled → "painting terms")*
>
> I allow myself eddies of meaning:  
> yield to a direction of significance  
> meaning  
> like a stream through the geography of my work:  
> you can find  
> in my sayings swerves of action  
> like the inlet's cutting edge:  
> there are dunes of motion,  
> organizations of grass, white sandy paths of remembrance  
> in the overall wandering of **mirroring** *(circled)* mind
>
> in nature there are few sharp lines: there are areas of  
> primrose  
> more or less dispersed;  
> disorderly orders of bayberry; between the rows  
> of dunes,  
> irregular swamps of reeds  
> though not reeds alone, but grass, bayberry, yarrow, all...  
> predominantly **reeds** *(boxed)*
>
> I have reached no conclusions, have created no boundaries,  
> shutting out and shutting in, **separating inside**  
> **from outside**: I have  
> **drawn no lines** → as

## Margin & Bracket Annotations
* **Left Vertical Margin:** `"— ekphrasis of place —"`
* **Middle Bracket (next to *"mirroring mind"*):** `"Kant p. 10 — even mirror is a representation etc. etc."` → `[mirror this environment in words & phrases the way a painter would mirror w/ strokes & colors —]`
* **Lower Right (next to *"predominantly reeds"*):** `"- is this about naming. He can pick the view apart, naming each plant or generalize. Paint it all in w/ one stroke."`
* **Bottom Right (below *"separating inside from outside"*):** `"→ what was my thought here? / mirror vs. window: in mind vs. real world."`
