# Someone Made This

I am not that sensitive being.
I want every one to be that sensitive.

People look at a painting differently b/c they imagine the artist's hand having glided `[gliding written above]` into every section of it.
The world of mechanical reproduction & compiled systems makes us forget; well lose the thought that someone made this.

by building working functional unique replicas you regain (?) that

It doesn't make sense to say that you can climb all the way back the industrial ladder to make a computer out of potatoes that you grew and metal that you smolded
