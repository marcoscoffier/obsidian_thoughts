# Dream of Showing Hull a Four-Wall Cityscape Room (Page 95)

*(Circled dream entry at bottom of page 95, linked visually to the painted-room sketches above it):*

I had this crazy dream about bringing Hull in to see a room where I had painted a cityscape on all 4 walls. I had built the whole room, you could see the frame work on the outside. I hadn't told anyone what I was doing. I wasted incredible numbers of hours doing it. I wasn't even taking painting, and here it was. It was crazy of me to have done this. It was too much, I had sacrificed too many other things but here it was, and I was looking for acceptance. I was laughing out of nervousness and excitement. It was wonderful.
