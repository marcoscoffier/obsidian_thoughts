# Only Happy When Working; a Portable Viewing Stand

2/27. My life has been miserable. The only time I'm happy is when I'm working. Incentive to go and do. I am only happy when working on my own stuff though, not @ rehersal or at OX meetings. Must be making my mind consuming stuff.
Had an idea about making a portable viewing stand which I could set up for any environment - time. I was thinking of engraving lines of poetry on the rail where you put your hands. Engraved like the lines on Wordsworth's Yew Tree.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Wordsworth's "Lines left upon a Seat in a Yew-tree" (1798) were meant to be read at a seat overlooking a view. The stamps pasted at the foot of the page belong with [[1995-02-21_The-Stamp-on-My-Last-Letter-to-Katherine]].
