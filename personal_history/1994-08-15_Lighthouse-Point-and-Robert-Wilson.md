# Lighthouse Point, Robert Wilson & Memory

I remember sitting on a bench at Lighthouse Point with Jimmy, who introduced me to Wilson's work, and who has read everything Yale owns on him; we sat facing the setting sun watching a seagull w/ a terribly deformed leg—when was he going to move? Could he walk or hop? The tide was rising, the sun was sinking, and two small Black children were chasing after other birds, trying to hit them w/ rocks, not laughing or smiling, just creeping up on pigeons & seagulls. Fortunately for the birds, though not for passersby, they had bad aim. Once again I was chilled by a horror of unseen, but felt violence in the inner city. What is forming these children's *(inserted: as of yet)* earliest memories?
