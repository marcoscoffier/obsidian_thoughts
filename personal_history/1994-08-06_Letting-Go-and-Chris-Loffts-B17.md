# Letting Go in the Mountains & Chris Lofft's B-17

I think I am way too hung up on what the artwork looks like. I would be in a much better situation if I had fun making—if I let myself go. I have been getting so hung up because I am feeling like my images are so important, rather than enjoying making them. I should run with the ideas of mixing stuff into the paint.

Eventually I will make something which someone will fixate upon and love the way Chris Lofft fixated on the B-17 model I made. I will never know exactly what he loved about it, or why he picked it up every time he came over even very late after we were in college, but I know that plane was made at the height of my model-building activities.
