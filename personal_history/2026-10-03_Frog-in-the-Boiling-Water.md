# Frog in the Boiling Water

I’m not doing well. I don’t want to go home. There is nowhere for me to go.

I feel like when I was a child—confused, vaguely angry, and lost.

I don’t want to be the frog in the boiling water. I see what is happening. It's just when I’m out of the pot, I’m lost. Everything I know and have built is in the pot. I don’t want to be in the pot or out. I don’t know what I want. I just know I’m not happy.
