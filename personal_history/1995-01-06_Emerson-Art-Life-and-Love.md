# Emerson: Art, Life and Love

If art = life then it doesn't by any means guarantee happiness.
Emerson's 'eternal picture which nature paints in the street' is not happy or good or pleasant. It can very easily be horrible, or unpleasant. Does this art movement remove emotion from any moment in life? Has humanity just hyped up emotions raised the good and ~~just~~ made the bad worse when it could just live at a pleasant flatline espoused by spirituals ~~[?]~~ many religions?
Why did katherine and I go so crazy over each other? do we both have a wish to be so happy or is it a natural human desire? Are we addicted to passion?
Why is there joy in the peceptual moments I mentioned a page back and which Emerson leads the way in saying once they are noticed one doesn't need art anymore?
Even Emerson seems to say there is a joy in those moments, but what if there is not? - then can art be used to create happiness despite a forlorn nature.
"For the <u>time</u> <u>being</u> art objects have a particular office to fill. That office, Emerson was writing in 1841, was to "educate us to the perception of beauty." Art is not an end, but an education. "What we have loved, others will love, and we will teach them how," Wordsworth - whom Emerson shares more with than with Plato - had ~~completed~~ concluded his life-work. p. 13-14.
Emerson: Selected writings Donald McQuade (NY Random 1981)
"Art"
`[a pink highlighter line across the page]`
Emerson's theory clearly implies a communication of a love, and this is where the issue lies w/ modern art w/ the Pop artist's and the Cage's.
Does Warhol love his Brillo boxes & Campbell soup cans? If I don't initially can I ever learn to?
Cage cannot, certainly, be guaranteed of loving every performance of 4'33" unless he is completely indifferent to sounds and has substituted indifference for love. `[the words "performance", "indifference", "for" and "love" are circled and joined]`
It is impossible to love at all times (?) Does love imply an elevated state? and thus that there is an equally depreciated state to balance things out? ~~or~~ or can this dialectic be abolished Energy / Matter becomes one and the same. The exchanged 'flatline' is acceptable (I cannot say one is content w/ etc.) ~~[?]~~ compared to the roller coaster. One picks one or the other. Bhuddism has picked the former. Western capitalist culture (lottery driven) has picked the latter.
I am wondering whether it would be possible to write an essay where one is not sure of the end result - like the best process of painting. I have a feeling that that is what Bachelard did ~~and~~ though he seems to have edited his notes to fit what looks like a finished orderly system of thoughts. He completes so many tangents though, which are the moments I most appreciate. I would like it if my essay could come directly from these notes where I am learning as I go, finding what I want to say and what I think as I go. This is a healthy creative process.
