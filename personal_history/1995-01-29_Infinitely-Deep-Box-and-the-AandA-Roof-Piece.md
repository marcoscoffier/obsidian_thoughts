# Infinitely Deep Box and the A&A Roof Piece

I just thought of a space which ~~[?]~~ seems infinitely deep - painted box so that the edges read as flat - or one is confused by what one is percieving.
↳ try this model `[a double line with an arrow runs down beside the next lines]`
\- the transparent mirror poses no confusion but both point - or do they?
The mirror points to infinite space, but also to flatness the infinite is contained yet present.
The ganz? field is what it is: it doesn't point.
\- When Turrell manipulates the sky is he using it to point to something else, or a ~~part of~~ certain a quality which it already possesses?
\- Amy pointed to the sky - hung things on it, I liked that.
I feel that I am slipping back into that world of books & uncertain places where I am so happy - where k. and I met and from which I have been at a remove.
