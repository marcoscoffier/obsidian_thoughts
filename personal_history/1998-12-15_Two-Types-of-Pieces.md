# Two Types of Pieces

Two types of pieces: ① those that marvel through some kind of trickery ← `[bracketed:]` smoke & magic
② those that lead to the deepest revelations but in a way that can be passed by. - holes in a tube - come to represent a certain constellation b/c the experience of that constellation in those holes is so rare. `[in the margin:]` (Nancy Holt)

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated. Nancy Holt's *Sun Tunnels* (1973–76) are concrete tubes in the Utah desert pierced with holes in the pattern of constellations.
