# The Nobel Prize, Genius, and Fate

[ - Nobel prize on works in genetics that proves the innate unbelievable similarity between human beings. ]
The Nobel prize picks a certain genius - like a star who is just like the rest of us. Einstein is a more pleasing character for fame than Bohr because he floats through the world - he forgets. Bohr is a family man who made his own institution.
`[an arrow from the margin:]` → a sort of subversion of the idea of the Nobel - of rewarding outstandingness
<u>genius / monster</u>
<u>outstanding / rediculous</u>
`[to the right, with an arrow:]` <u>fate</u> how much does one really accomplish beyond what one's situation has decided?
