# Thoughts During a Reading of *Languages of Art*

Make a room, or set of walls with openings—make them nicely with drywall, sharp corners, putty, painted. Surround each opening with a picture frame (only on one side). Could I use one-way mirrors? From one side people make their own image; from the other they see the same.

My other idea: a box, a setup still life (with Playmobil?). Two openings in the box: on one end have a picture frame with an empty opening inside to see the actual setup; from the other, have a photograph of the setup from that side. 

What about two-way mirrors, one on each side with the mirror side facing inwards? How about an entire box with all sides made with one-way mirrors facing inwards—you see an infinite space of which you are not a part. Could I put an object inside these reflections? Another form of infinity: a frame, painting of a frame, of a photograph of a framed painting of a photograph.
