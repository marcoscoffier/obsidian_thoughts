# Dance Space and Mirror Panels

`[sketch: a floor plan of a long room, with measurements: "7'", "7'", "3ft", "7'", "7'" along the top, "15'", "7.5'" in the middle, "30" on each side, "3 skinny" at each end, "6'5" and "10'9"" along the bottom, and "5 mirror panels" and "4 mirror panels" below; sums ("25", "66.5 - 15 = 51.5") in pencil]`
It is so interesting how dance is so much about the intricacies of the human body - what is possible? what can these dancer's bodies do? - like wrestling (kind of - my only frame of referance) but the interaction btw. these two individuals is not about the space - go from the most neutral black space - to space w/ defined polarization - to one much larger which is again not polarized.
should they use the largest space? they can be <u>v. far</u> away from the audience & then inside / amongst them
we can put the mirrors as far apart as we can - get reflection w/ mirror L's
