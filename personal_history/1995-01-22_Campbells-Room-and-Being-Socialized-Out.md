# Campbell's Room and Being Socialized Out

I went to Campbell's room tonight - fun lot's of laughter. I realize that what I had taken as laziness in the '94 class always using the same expression, is really a new form of signification. The phrase, whatever it is: "flower penis" "the juice goes" etc. represents an entire story the essence of that story and the cameraderie felt at the moment and through the repetition of the phrase are captured in the word, which is repeated carefully for best effect.

**Next page:**

I am socialized out. Having had too much fun this weekend I need to spend some time working stuff out alone. This is the most distant I have felt from katherine and my projects ~~[?]~~ yet this school year. It feels really good to hang out w/ these people I never would have met - I don't have to act anything around them and yet I am surprised at how I am acting - is this me?. Is the me I had built - the one I lost in ~~[?]~~ pangs of shame about my pretensiousness the week before giving my bio. gone to a new distilled me - free of some excess
I think it is too bad that I am no longer painting regularly the pictures are no longer a record of how I have been feeling What would I paint like now if I could be as free as I was in the night club in NY?
