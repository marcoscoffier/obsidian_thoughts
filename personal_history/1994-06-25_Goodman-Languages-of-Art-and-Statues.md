# Nelson Goodman’s *Languages of Art* & Alternating Statues Idea

* **Notebook Reference:** `Languages of Art: pgs. 7–8 (text) 18 diagram` (`see p. 59`).
* **Top Handwritten Installation Note:**
  > `"a row of statues, wood blocks [making a word] but alternate btw actual statues in nooks & representations of those same [statues]"`
* **Pasted Clippings (Nelson Goodman, *Languages of Art*):**
  * *"The catch here, as Ernst Gombrich insists, is that there is no innocent eye. The eye comes always ancient to its work, obsessed by its own past and by old and new insinuations of the ear, nose, tongue, fingers, heart, and brain... It does not so much mirror as take and make; and what it takes and makes it sees not bare, as items without attributes, but as things, as food, as people, as enemies, as stars, as weapons. Nothing is seen nakedly or naked."*
  * *"The myths of the innocent eye and of the absolute given are unholy accomplices... what has been received and what has been done to it cannot be distinguished within the finished product."*
