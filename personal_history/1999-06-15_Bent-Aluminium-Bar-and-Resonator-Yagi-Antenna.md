# Bent Aluminium Bar and Resonator; Yagi Antenna

~~bent~~ aluminium `[above:]` bar bent to isolate and visualise the vibrations which become sound waves
resonator - box with an 'empty volume and opening such that the volume of air vibrates in sympathy w/ the bar and to amplify these sound waves.
