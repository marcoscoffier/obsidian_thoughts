# Canopy of Leaves & James Turrell's Light Spaces

After having concentrated all my thoughts on the road, my legs, and my breathing [while running], I lay down to rest under a large tree. I let my eyes unfocus—or rather go into a kind of general focus—where I could see all the leaves in the canopy above me fluttering in the wind, sharp specks of black against the bright evening sky, pulsing to the beat of my heartbeat. I want to go to the beach tomorrow and paint on a flagstone and stick sand on my painting where there is sand and coast—perhaps this is too forced, like Ashbery's *"The Painter."*

I read today about James Turrell's space and light sculptures. I love them. The man is doing things that are so big. They tap into the beginning of humanity's artistic mission—the awe of the elements. My small box with mirrors is just that: small. It connotes a jewel, or minerals hidden in small spaces in the rocks underground.
