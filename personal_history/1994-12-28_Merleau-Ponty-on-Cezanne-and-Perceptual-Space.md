# Merleau-Ponty on Cézanne and Perceptual Space

I am very excited by my 1st foray into Merleau-Ponty on Cezanne - It is hard to focus on the text because of my excitement. I keep picturing making a huge canvas 180° again drawing w/ the paint building it as I go, not caring what it looks like <u>trusting that I can pull it through in the end.</u> `[right, bracketed:]` That is all important. - I never feel lost in a drawing like I do in a painting
Important 1st note:
In ~~classical~~ modern Western Philosophy science & Art the point, line, plane & ratio are given 1st place of importance from Plato on → Leonardo, Galileo, Descartes.
2ndary are: color, taste, sound, touch, odor
`[bracketed in the margin:]` "Ponty sought to restore the solidity of the lived-qualities of the world especially color and the tangible thickness of things, depth understood not as a linear third dimension constructed as an illusion, but primordial depth as the "most existential dimension" - p. 12 Reader
↳ so important - this is what I want to talk about a new concept of space - ~~[?]~~ - perceptual -
Cezanne is bridging the gap w/ the impressionists & ~~[?]~~ wanting the shapes to be solid. Maybe I am bridging the gap w/ Turrel etc. bringing back some geometry - transcendence.
" [Classical artists] created pictures; we are attempting a piece of nature." - Cezanne
Nature: " the artist must conform to this perfect work of art. Everything comes to us from nature; we exist through it; nothing else is worth remembering. "
