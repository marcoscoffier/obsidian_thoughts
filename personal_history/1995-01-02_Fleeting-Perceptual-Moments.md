# Fleeting Perceptual Moments

1/2/95
It is really quite wonderful to be aware of all the fleeting perceptual moments in life: A door's shadow and reflection on the floor w/ light diffracting on either side making it look like a triangle; or more special: the blue glow caught between two white walls or how the Raw/Burnt Sienna window sill in my room became a flame of cad red in the corner of my eye beside the blue outside and the softer blue reflected wall.
