# Hull & His Son Drawing as Equals, Plus Sky Painting Tips (Page 81)

`*` Before coming over Hull went by the comic store to buy his kid some comics that he could read in the car on the way to Baltimore: *"We bond through drawing"* Hull said, *"He loves drawing super-heroes (Thats all I drew when I was young - Hull)"*. He showed me a small sketch book he had taken to the ballgame with his son. *"He could never sit through a whole game, he doesn't understand it, but if we're drawing he'll stay 3 hrs."* In the book the pages alternated Dad & son. *"With drawing we can be equals, I'm not teaching him anything. We're both artists."* A strange comment coming from a Yale professor. I know he's dying to have his son become an artist which was hard for him & will be for me (if I decide to do it) because it was so foreign to what our parents did. It will be absolutely natural for Hull's son — both his parents are artists, And he will have been drawing the way I would listen to music w/ my mom, he's not even 5 yet. Those memories from before real memory begins are so powerful.

Before I forget, Hull had two tips about painting large flat planes: mix 3 big pots up first of maybe 3 sky colors, to have enough to cover whole sky & maybe I should paint those areas w/ shorter strokes in the quick paintings, I pick up my brush, whereas the longer ones are constantly scumbled. I have to find if the high detail multiple reflections is really for me or something more about the gesture — maybe both?
