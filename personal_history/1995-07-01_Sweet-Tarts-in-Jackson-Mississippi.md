# Sweet-Tarts in Jackson, Mississippi

Travelling is so wonderful, for some reason it frees your thoughts.

Pocahantas
Sweet-Tarts
Bought at Wal-Mart
In Jackson, Mississippi
More candy than I could ever eat myself
for ~~$~~ 68 ~~¢~~ cents
I got to share them with others
and watch the grimaces as
the Disney caracters were
cracked between teeth
painfull but fulfilling
I like that: Sweet-Tart

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated. Disney's *Pocahontas* came out in June 1995, which fits a summer 1995 trip to Jackson, Mississippi.
