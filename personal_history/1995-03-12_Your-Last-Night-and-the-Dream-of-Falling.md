# Your Last Night, and the Dream of Falling

`[in pencil:]` It is your last night You have been convicted of a crime you are not guilty of and condemned to death. The prison guards have allowed you one last wish, to run on an empty beach in the evening They have left you for your last few minutes of freedom.
Falling makes the decision for you. p. 138 "mythe..."
~~[?]~~ dream to fall is the completed death wish w/out the decision.
I wonder if it is the same impulse: when no one is there to help you when you are down - you have tons of friends when you are happy, as that no one will really miss you when you die Millions will live on for thousands of years.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated. The first paragraph is in pencil. "mythe" is probably Camus's *Le Mythe de Sisyphe*, which opens on suicide; the page reference is to Marco's edition. On falling, compare [[1995-03-12_Vertigo]].
