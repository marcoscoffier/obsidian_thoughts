# Perceptual and Conceptual Space

Group μ under metaxes.
anacoluthon → produces through bifurcation a break in sentance-structure.
Literature the time art leaves traces of the past -
~~Art~~ Painting the space art ponders this intangible, invisible unknowable.
Painter takes this space under control - Ponty quote about the body -
\- but can at best give a representation. ~~The~~ Closest another viewer can get to point of view of seer two heads looking @ mountain.
Painter of nature can ~~recreates~~ recreate his sight. And place it before your eyes.
His power lies in his ability to create this alterior reality.
the "reality of the museums" - Cezanne.
Perceptual Space → domain of the painter
Conceptual Space → that of the writer.
`[circled:]` Real vs. "Bachelard" → unknowable
`[circled:]` Imaginary → history, knowable or known, but untouchable.
touching the Madeliene unleashed Proust's <u>Rememberance</u>
contemplating the void in <u>Terre des Hommes</u> → unleashes
\- everything you can touch is an extension of yourself. in the present ↳ Ponty
→ everything else is an <u>other</u> Space and the past are lumped into a same category.
The other w/in yourself → dreams unconscious Breton
The others out side → here we start to unpack Bachelard's oxymoron: Intimate: Immencity.
odd conflation in French of sens `[continuing up the right margin, sideways:]` and sens. mentioned by Espace et Poesie conference and by Ponty in Phen. Percep.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated; essay notes that follow the April 11 entry. Groupe μ is the Belgian group of rhetoricians whose *Rhétorique générale* (1970) classes figures such as anacoluthon under "métataxes". Bachelard's "immensité intime" is a chapter of *La Poétique de l'espace*. "sens and sens" is the French *sens* as both "meaning" and "direction" (and "the senses"); see [[1995-03-12_Intellectual-Space]].
