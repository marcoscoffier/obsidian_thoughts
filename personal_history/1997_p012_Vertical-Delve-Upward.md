# Vertical — Delve Upward

`[sketch: a heavy vertical stroke beside the title]`
<u>Vertical</u>
\- delve upward -

as the wind settles
<u>dawn</u>   16
→ bridges of the city

→ height the imagination spans
beyond despair.

`[sketch: a horizontal bar of lines; under it a bare tree, with times written along arrows: "18. Helen[?] 50", "→ 11:00 ←", "patent[?]"]`
→ 15:30. And now.
15.35 → brazen[?]

→ <u>hiatus</u>
bargain
→ vocable? & prayer
→ lost yet poised in traffic

`[sketch: a tall glass box, its corners blacked in]` → grotest
dust
↳ <u>dusk</u>
→ yeilding cities
<u>flickering</u>

<u>Salut au Monde</u> `[sketch: a hammer-like mark before it, a circle after]`
I meet you 15:50.
foot to foot `[sketch: a vertical post with arrows swinging to two bars on either side]`
→ this music has a re-assuring way. ←
→ read & repeat ←
→ <u>delve</u> upward
`[sketch: a burst of lines falling from a point, like a fountain or a firework]`
[ slim skaters of the gardened skies ]

\- a goose tabacco & cologne

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Fragments of a poem or a score, with times of day. "Salut au Monde!" is the title of a Whitman poem. "Helen", "patent" and "brazen" are uncertain readings of small writing by the drawing. "a goose tabacco & cologne" and "flickering" are in a different blue pen, added later.
