# Change of Heart on Cy Twombly & Jackson Pollock

A key moment this year was my conversion of judgment on Cy Twombly—his strokes can be quite amazing, reminiscent of the delicate flowing Japanese cursive style (like a blackboard on which you can still see previous classes). At the same time, an early Pollock painting I saw at the Guggenheim moved me more than any work in memory (*Ocean Greyness*, 1953: in the actual painting it is much more obvious that the grey is painted on top of the colored stuff; he has selected out parts he liked and unified the whole).
