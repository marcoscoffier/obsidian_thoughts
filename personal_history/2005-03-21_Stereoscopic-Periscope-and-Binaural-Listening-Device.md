# Stereoscopic Periscope and Binaural Listening Device

`[pencil sketch: two parabolic dishes side by side labelled "ears", with two periscope tubes rising between them labelled "eyes"]`
2 ears, 2 eyes a stereoscopic periscope and binaural distance listening device
\- 2 large parabolic dishes w/ hoses at the focal points concentrate distant sounds into the end of a hose where the sounds are carried freely to the observer's ears
`[sketch: a dish with a hose curving round to a listener's ear]`
\- 2 pipes with lenses fitted into the ends bring a distant image to a screen which provides a stereoscopic image. Like a pinhole camera, but with the lense the image is much more bright.
`[sketch: a periscope tube with a "lense" at the top end, a "mirror" at each bend and a "screen" in front of a viewer's face]`
