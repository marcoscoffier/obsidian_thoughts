# A Blank Day

1/7/95 Well, as one could probably tell by the copious amounts of writing I ~~did~~ did in my journal, this has not been a very thought provoking day. I am hard pressed to think of a day when my mind was such a blank. At best I can think of this day as gathering reserves for the days to come which will be wonderful, I hope - anything to counter today's lack of every thing. I can't even call it the duldrums it was so blank, blah really. Perhaps my coffee will jump start my brain.
