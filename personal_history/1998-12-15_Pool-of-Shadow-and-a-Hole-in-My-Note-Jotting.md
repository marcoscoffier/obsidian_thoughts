# Pool of Shadow, and a Hole in My Note Jotting

<u>Thoughts</u> - "pool of shadow", I read in an old notebook.
The light falling from the enlarger onto the paper which you can block w/ your hands is actually very beautiful. I placed unsuccessfully a photo on plexi - suspended in the ~~a~~ vide above l'atelier du Manier so that it too would receive the light that fell on it.
It is unfortunate that there is such a huge hole in my note jotting in these copy books, I feel that my life these past two years has been particularly confused bearings shaken abit, but hey I'll hopefully avoid a mid-life crisis this way.
