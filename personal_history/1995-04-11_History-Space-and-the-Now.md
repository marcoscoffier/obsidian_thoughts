# History, Space and the Now

<u>Apr 11</u>.
Experience the now occurs on a plane
`[sketch: two lines crossing in an X, the left end labelled "History", the right end "space", with a dotted vertical line through the crossing labelled "now"]`
like standing on the middle of a palindrome (palimsest?)
<u>bab</u> seeing ob in
<u>civic</u>
anapodoton. exemplified by the Princeton Encycl. under Ellipsis. "If you do it all will be well, if not `[circled:]` ..."
`[under "If you do it all will be well", underlined:]` History `[under the circled "...":]` space the unknowns the future
`[to the right:]` <u>Chiasmus</u>.
replace the space inside with that which is outside massive dreamspace.
Giacometti ~~Max Ernst~~ quote: ~~roof of~~ immense vault of a human head.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> "bab" is written over "bob"; the point is the "o" or "a" at the centre of a palindrome, as "v" in "civic". Anapodoton (a sentence left unfinished) is defined under "Ellipsis" in the *Princeton Encyclopedia of Poetry and Poetics*. The Giacometti quotation was first attributed to Max Ernst, then corrected.
