# Stasis and Commitment

You always need to ruin things - Alison tells me.
I hate stasis, nothing scares me more.
Want new adventure - being on the edge in whatever way and yet there are levels of ~~[?]~~ caring that imply a commitment which requires a limit ~~on~~ new experience.
katherine, you've got to know how much I love you - but like you felt last year - are you the only person I love? No that would be dangerous. Do I love you most? As far as I can tell.
