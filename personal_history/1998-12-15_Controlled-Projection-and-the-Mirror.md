# Controlled Projection and the Mirror

`[sketch: a gallery room in perspective: a projector on the ceiling, a sloping beam to a small device on a stand, a hatched box, a small screen or panel on the wall, and a standing figure facing it]`
`[in red:]` controlled projection
`[written diagonally across the lower half of the page:]`
\- crawl into another person's mind. body.
\- sometimes when you stand before a mirror it is a difficult jump to say that person is me.
To demonstrate this realisation it is not enough to hang a mirror in a gallery - though the situation of a ~~[?]~~ person before a mirror is so particular.
