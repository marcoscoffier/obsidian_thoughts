# A Way of Seeing, and Missing Katherine

March 6th
The painting classes taught a way of seeing more than they did anything else. I carry this with me every day it colors my expeience of the real world this is more powerful than the moment of seeing before a painting.
just like aging, beautiful weathering can't be faked. I would want to build a weathered barn - but no one can. It is better just to find it.
I missed katherine terribly last night. It would be so good to see her even if we are broken up.
