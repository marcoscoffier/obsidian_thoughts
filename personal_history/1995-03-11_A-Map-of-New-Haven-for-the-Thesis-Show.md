# A Map of New Haven for the Thesis Show

3/11 I am going to make my portable stand - and set it up for six weeks detailing every spot on ~~[?]~~ where it sits ~~[?]~~ ~~first~~ then to present these @ the Thesis show I want to get an enourmous `[boxed:]` map of New Haven which would include all these sites and I will beef each spot up w/ pictures drawings and descriptions of the light situation, and have the box on the roof of the A&A or of Chapel York - where the two projects can converge. -
We
open our eyes and ears seeing life
each day excellent as it is
This realization no longer needs art.
\- John Cage.
Alas! Ages must pass away before men will have their eyes open to the beauty and majesty of Truth, and will be taught to venerate Poetry no further than as she is a handmaid....
Up! up! my friend, and quit your books.... - William Wordsworth
I believe any sensible person would change his pictures, however good, for windows - John Ruskin.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> The portable stand is the one in [[1995-02-27_Only-Happy-When-Working-a-Portable-Viewing-Stand]]; the box on the roof is [[1995-03-09_Rooftop-AandA-Piece-Microcosm-Box]]. "Chapel York" is probably the corner of Chapel and York Streets, near the A&A. "Up! up! my friend, and quit your books" opens Wordsworth's "The Tables Turned".
