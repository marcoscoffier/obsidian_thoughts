# Rudolf Arnheim on Originality vs. Personal Style (Page 104)

* **Top Handwritten Quote (Rudolf Arnheim, *Art and Visual Perception*, p. 138):**
  > `"Originality is the unsought and unnoticed product of a gifted [?] artist's successful attempt to be honest and truthful, to penetrate to the origins [modern], the roots, of what he sees. The deliberate search for a personal style inevitably interferes w/ the validity of the work, because it introduces an element of arbitrariness into a process that can be governed only by necessity." — p. 138.`
