# Thoughts on Semiotics: Signifiers & Context

When I called Garth this weekend he wasn't home:
"Hi, this is Marco."
"Hi, it's Amy."
Doesn't this message tell me anything? I did not know of course which Amy it was—the only two girls in the apartment are called Amy. Garth is talking about the two girls named Amy whom he lives with; without thinking about it, he uses the same signifier. She also mentioned the McDonald's Quarter Pounder—in places where they don't measure things in pounds [e.g., *Royale with Cheese* / metric countries], you don't always connect the name "Quarter Pounder" with the fact that it is a burger with 1/4 lb of meat, but rather it stands for this particular McDonald's sandwich and its look or wrapping.
