# Dream of a Professor and a Loaded Word

The other day I ~~thought~~ dreamt ~~actually~~ of a proffessor who as he tried to approach a discussion of a character in a book whos name was Tina-bitch. Tried to use this term in the way I felt it was implied in the story as a jokey insult - the way two women, or homosexuals may call each other bitch, but because of how loaded the term is and because the character was black he could not get out of what had become a racial blunder.
The word, the lexical item, was so loaded that he could not defuse it with context or for the purpose of discussion - its mere utterance branded him as whiteman / racist.
