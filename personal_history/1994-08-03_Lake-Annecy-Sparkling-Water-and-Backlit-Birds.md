# Sitting on a Bench at Lake Annecy: Sparkling Water & Birds (Page 101)

I sat on a bench in Annecy looking at the lake from under the shade of a large tree, finally I could content myself after a long day of being w/ a group who flirted w/ experience rather than partake in it. The sun was in such a position that I could not see it, but its presence was easily percieved by the glowing outlines of some branches and leaves above me and by the flashes of light, with the sparkling feel of a discoteche casion [casino?] or christmas tree, off the ever changing water, which I thought looked like tight Saran wrap, then turmoiled mercury — then nothing so specific or so muscular. The changing nature of the waves affected the flashing sun on its surface from the frenetic excitement of a disco to erratic twinkling of an elderly matron's sequined robe. The birds would react to the changes of the wind, waves and the sun which all changed each other. When the backlit birds would land, their initial impact w/ the water would be followed by a sparkling ring of sun.

> *(Right margin, sideways):* `this is an effect to be captured w/ a brush or a camera.`
