# Showing a Work I Didn't Build

What does it mean if I decide to show a work I didn't build, didn't even touch didn't even ask to have built?
HB says he prefers "Corson's Inlet" the poem to the place what If I just lead you to the place?
I could try to recreate, but isn't that a representation?
Should I make labels on the fence? Like going to the forest and labeling a tree - less even than Goldsworthy who ~~[?]~~ would spend hours in a place battling w/ and discovering the forces that are there.
But more than conceptual artists who would not even complete the ~~plans for the~~ pieces they had made plans for.
I need to take pictures. Make a drawing?
There & on the A&A roof.
