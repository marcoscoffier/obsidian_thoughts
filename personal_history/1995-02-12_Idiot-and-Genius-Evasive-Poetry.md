# Idiot and Genius; "Evasive Poetry"

Am I searching for something greater than the individual classes are looking for, or am I just washing them fooling myself - the line between an idiot & a genius is so fine.
`[circled, under "idiot", with an arrow:]` a fool → now Shakespeare has changed the meaning of this word.
"evasive poetry"
\- art is a whole other language

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated; between the Ashbery reading ([[1995-02-12_John-Ashbery-Reading]]) and 2/15. "evasive poetry" may refer to Ashbery. "washing them" may be a slip for "wasting them".
