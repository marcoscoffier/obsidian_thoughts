# Back Cover: Talks, and a Wedding Card Draft

`[top left, upright:]`
Apr. 18 Chuck Close / Robert Storr. 6.30
Apr. 19 Steve Reich 7.00
`[in the middle of the back cover, written upside down and at a slant:]`
Dear S & R, ~~[?]~~ I wish you All the best ~~[?]~~ ~~wishes~~ for your new life together. I hope to see you two in D.C. or Baltimore this summer and in the years to come.
I framed a drawing I made of Jeff freshman year. I thought it would make sense to give it to you when you are in Baltimore, so you wouldn't have to transport it.
love
Marco

> [!note] Transcription notes
> The inside back cover. The two talks are probably Yale events in April 1995 (18 and 19 April). The upside-down note is a draft of a wedding card to a couple, "S & R"; the crossed-out word after "Dear S & R" and the word after "best" are hard to read. Jeff is Jeff Tuder, Marco's room-mate from freshman to junior year.
