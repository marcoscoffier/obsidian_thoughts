# Falling for Katherine & Her Edge (8/1)

I didn't really think about why I was falling for Katherine, but it makes so much sense. It is something about me, liking the non-skirt wearer, lumberjack aspiring cheese-avoider Katherine. It's weird but I am proud to think that I can call her my girlfriend. She has such an edge over most other women. I don't know how she did it.
