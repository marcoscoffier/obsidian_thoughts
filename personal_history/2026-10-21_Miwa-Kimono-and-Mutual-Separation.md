# Miwa, the Graduation Kimono & Mutual Separation

But I don’t want to keep secrets from my special someone. Mi feels burdened by my troubles. She often accuses me of bringing her down. I want a partner and the support to attack the world together. We mentioned this difference of opinion in couples therapy.

I felt especially off when for our son’s graduation, instead of a cool artsy outfit from one of her designer friends, she chose to wear a traditional kimono. Rather than celebrate the cool artist mom she was to our son, she hid behind the uniform of Japanese-ness. Every other parent and teacher saw her only through this lens. She often says that she isn’t the same person from when she was young. But I didn’t sign up for a tradwife from Japan. I’m not fond of that strict sexless person.

Spoke with Miwa on Sunday and got her to agree to see a marriage counselor; set up the meeting for next Sunday.

I tried to speak with Miwa about a mutual separation where we both find happiness—she got so angry she was hitting me. Extricating myself will be such a mess. What's so hard is I tried to be an ally, to support the household in so many ways. I really want to see myself as the supportive head of family, loved and respected. A separation to become another loser divorced dad really hurts my image—who I want to be, how I want to be in the world. I’m quite lost really.
