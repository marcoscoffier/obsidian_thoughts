# Crête de l'Aiguille Percée: Wind-Sound Bottle Sculpture (Page 90v)

I thought of making an object which makes sound. Today on top of the **Crête de l'Aiguille Percée** I opened a bottle of Badoit (it was already half empty) and the wind blew across the opening & made a hollow sound so...

> *[Diagrams: Spherical hollow vessel suspended on vertical posts with directional wind vanes (`<- wings that keep it in line w/ the wind`), revised to a circumferential lip/saucer (`wing should be a saucer that way wind can come from any side`), with side and perspective saucer sketches.]*

- this would need help from scientists
- another Apfel question
- make different ones that produce different sounds — a chord?
