# The Christian Paradox

Most educated Christians don't believe in the physical reality of Christ as God's son, which is fine. The religion still holds together on the tenet that it is spreading the word of the Lord—the message in the stories. I ask: why do we all have to go through the elaborate charade of convincing each other that we believe, when in fact we don't? You might say that by forcing everyone to follow the word, you get fringe people to do things—good things—which they would not have done had there not been this elaborate charade. 

I'm glad I do know something of these stories, so that the church is a common reference frame for much of the Western world. The problems I have with most church services are the same that I have with a lot of mass culture in general.
