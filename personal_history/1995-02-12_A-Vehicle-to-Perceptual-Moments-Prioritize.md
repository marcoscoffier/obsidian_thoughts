# A Vehicle to Perceptual Moments; Prioritize!

~~I am trying to capture~~
I feel that the most exciting visual effects I would notice while painting are ones which lose their effect in a representation. I am trying to recreate or capture or simply frame these actual visuals in a way where they can become accessible to my viewer
`[bracketed:]` I am not so interested in objects as I am in the relationships between them - How different colored lights mix in their shadows. how light reflects through different surfaces.
I want to become a vehicle for the viewer to these perceptual moments.
<u>PRIORITIZE!</u>
I need major documentation - photographs & sketches not of the pieces as much as of the data I want
I should photograph the fence - prepare the "frame"
<u>mental path = frame</u> for fence piece - (fleeting purple)
\- I give my viewers a map.
\- either the least egotistical or the most egotistical thing an artist can do.
\- take pictures from the roof of A&A & finish a drawing
<u>complete a proposal</u> - which can be included in the show even if I do not build it
\- my affinity for conceptual art meets my love of nature & visuals.
`[bracketed:]` I should make the T.M. box w/ the light as a demo - the switch pull makes the effect. → <u>miniature</u>
