## [[1994-06-07_Painting-Idea-Infinite-Reflection|Painting Idea: Infinite Reflection]]
*1994-06-07 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 31 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p050.jpg|p. 50]], [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p052.jpg|p. 52]]*

Onto the most exciting thoughts I had today: I was picturing a painting of the artist's portrait with an eye of the subject blown up on the curved reflecting surface—or two such paintings: one of the artist's reflection, the second of the artist's eye with the reflection of the subject. If these two paintings were set up facing each other in a viewing space at a distance appropriate to their size, the viewer could become the same size as the reflections and could thus be associated with either the artist or the subject. He would somehow be stepping into the imaginary rays of reflection between the paintings.

It belongs to the situation which would occur in the event of a self-portrait: a double-sided mirror could be placed between the two eyes to maximize the size of the reflection. Would it be possible to repeat this infinite mirroring even if the viewer couldn't actually see it—reduced onto a slide or microfilm?
