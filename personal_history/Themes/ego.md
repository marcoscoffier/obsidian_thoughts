## [[1994-07-28_Reversal-During-Hulls-Studio-Visit|The Reversal During Hull's Visit (Page 82)]]
*1994-07-28 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 82 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_2_p009.jpg|p. 9]]*

It is incredible the type of reversal that occured when Hull came to look at my paintings. I was no longer knowledgeable, experienced and giving advice like I had been w/ Omar, or the senior who would pop into drawing class & make paintings. I felt like I really didn't have very much to show for all the excitement I had had. Hull mentioned None of the special revelations I had had, the red shadow on the door in Alison's room, the surface of the rock making clouds. He did mention the green shirt next to the red ear—he had seen it happen & was impressed that day of the big-ego boost—Nothing else moved him like that. Once again I can't help but feel that painting doesn't ever directly translate, people don't just fall in love w/ your work, or a work until they know something to make it mean something to them. For me it is something `[right margin, sideways: first or the making.]`

## [[1999-06-15_Genius-in-Science-and-in-Art|Genius in Science and in Art]]
*1999-06-15 (estimated) · Paris, Beaux-Arts · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1997_2_p072.jpg|p. 72]]*

In science genius needs no masquerade one can say one is a scientist - it is a daily activity, a result of successful completion of a type of education. Too often I confound the word artist w/ the word genius where I should say an artist of genius. In fact only an artist of genius is interesting. An artist "tout court" is pathetic, no use, no interest. This for me is a fundamental problem in my manuvering in my small art world. And is a fundamental chauvanism I must get over or hide.
\_\_\_
My idea of genius is closer to the scientist's I think. Many people are working for a similar goal, sharing ideas & results after the fact, or through constant interaction one person is considered to be a genius by the others
The results, the advances are elsewhere.
