## [[1997_p033_Someone-Made-This|Someone Made This]]
*1997 · Paris, Beaux-Arts · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1997_p033.jpg|p. 33]]*

I am not that sensitive being.
I want every one to be that sensitive.

People look at a painting differently b/c they imagine the artist's hand having glided `[gliding written above]` into every section of it.
The world of mechanical reproduction & compiled systems makes us forget; well lose the thought that someone made this.

by building working functional unique replicas you regain (?) that

It doesn't make sense to say that you can climb all the way back the industrial ladder to make a computer out of potatoes that you grew and metal that you smolded

> [!note]- Transcription notes
> Written in pencil. "(?)" after "regain" is on the page. "smolded" is as written (smelted).

## [[1998-12-15_Sky-Shadow-Pieces-and-Embarrassment|Sky Shadow Pieces, and Embarrassment]]
*1998-12-15 (estimated) · Paris, Beaux-Arts · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1997_2_p055.jpg|p. 55]]*

I think of the Sky shadow pieces and the intense embarassement I felt when setting them up - but also sureness of what I was doing. All of the workmanship imitating an industrial production in fact is an attempt to make the special moment I am framing seem absolutely normal. There is beauty in ~~that~~ normalcy. In the sky shadow piece: building the frame was a way of protecting this simple idea - protecting it from those who would laugh it off. this protection is somewhat tennuous for it may be even stupider to say look at that guy who spent so long building that thing - but that time validates an action `[an arrow from "spent", written up the margin:]` gives it valeur

> [!note]- Transcription notes
> Undated. "Sky Shadow" (also "Sun Shadow") and "Sky Color" were outdoor pieces from Marco's last year at Yale (1995).

## [[1999-06-15_Grounding-Laisser-les-Gens-Faire|Grounding: Laisser les Gens Faire]]
*1999-06-15 (estimated) · Paris, Beaux-Arts · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1997_2_p079.jpg|p. 79]]*

Grounding.
les vues les plus interesante d'une ville ou un endroit sont <u>imprevus</u>
il est impossible de planifiée toutes les vues de tout les utilisateurs d'un endroit qui font l'image de cette endroit.
Laisser les gens faire. Ils ont la meilleur idée de ce qui leur convient.
Il faut leur donné les moyens → bricks, connaissance de pouvoir utilisé des materiaux moins chers - contre industrie du service
Je peux le faire mieux pour <u>moi</u> si je m'occupe de moi, ma maison, ma cuisine.
c'est la force qu'on reve que tient l'artiste le manuel, le technitien, il peut faire, il est libre.
[Webber, M. - Urbanist.]

> [!note]- Transcription notes
> Undated, in blue ink. Melvin Webber was an American urban planner ("The Urban Place and the Nonplace Urban Realm", 1964). The ideas echo Dubuffet's "remettre à chaque citoyen du ciment et une truelle" in [[1998-12-15_Dubuffet-Psycho-Sites-and-the-Hydra]].
