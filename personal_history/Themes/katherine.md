## [[1994-06-20_Katherines-Badlands-Postcard|Katherine's Badlands Postcard]]
*1994-06-20 · Summer 1994, Travel and Alps · scan [[attachments/pages/postcard_1994_katherine_front.jpg|postcard_1994_katherine_front]], [[attachments/pages/postcard_1994_katherine_back.jpg|postcard_1994_katherine_back]]*

`[front: Badlands National Park — a cracked white mud flat in the foreground, green and dry brush in the middle, and a ridge of sharp orange-red peaks against a pale blue sky]`

`[back, printed:]` BADLANDS NATIONAL PARK. #14727 G-2/17 Mud cracks in a dry wash illustrate the semiarid climate and highly erosive nature of Badlands rocks—two factors that help to create the spectacular Badlands scenery. Photographer—Carr Clifton

`[address:]` Marco Scoffier / PO Box 4191 Yale Station / New Haven, CT 06520 / - AIR MAIL -

June 20 - 1AM Seattle WA youth hostel
Dear Marco, Lisa & I had our first shower since Minnesota & finally brushed our teeth. Went crazy sleeping at rest stops in the car for 3 nights in a row across the Dakota's, Wyoming, & Montana. Thought you'd appreciate this postcard. <u>oh!</u>, the land out west is just waiting for you, Marco!
2 things: make sure you catch the next Letterman Show w/ the 2 Pakistani men at Mt. Rushmore. Lisa & I were there which made the tourist attraction all the more exciting!

## [[1994-12-28_Flavors-of-Life-and-Katherine|Flavors of Life and Katherine]]
*1994-12-28 · Final Semester Yale 1995 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1995_p002.jpg|p. 2]]*

12/28/94
I love how life has so many different flavors. I never could have imagned sensations like I had on the trip visiting katherine or like I just had listening to Dvorak's Requiem. I find the flavors come in books and music or paintings or people each of those four is a means of communicating the same type of disarming molding and manipulation of your soul. You have to let your self be openned up - splayed like I felt on my bed listening to Dvorak after the exhausting emotional turmoils of not going w/ the SOB's on mario's insane amounts of driving tour and the complicated manneuvering of our lives katherine & I are going to under take for the next couple years 'till we can both settle down to something, hopefully together. I think that we are both people who will continue to change for our entire lives - I don't think we will ever be content in any kind of stasis. It makes me love katherine so much when she couldn't just waste time in Berkeley to be with me. We are happiest with each other and ourselves when we are forging ahead. I hope we can find a situation when we can forge ahead together - where our goal might be common - that could be for in the future or never. will we ever have found ourselves enough to unite in that way? Is such a union possible or feasable or ~~wanted~~ to be desired?.
We share something I don't know if it can be called love It certainly is closer to love than anything else, but so unusual, different and personal - a feeling which could only happen between us two that I hesitate to classify it with the generic hackneyed name, 'love'.
I'm very excited to write in this beautiful book with the hole in the first page.

> [!note]- Transcription notes
> The date could also be read 12/29/94. "The SOB's" are probably the Yale Society of Orpheus and Bacchus, an a cappella group that tours over the winter break; "mario" is not identified. The "hole in the first page" is visible on the page as a small punched hole.

## [[1995-01-13_Uncomfortable-in-Groups|Uncomfortable in Groups]]
*1995-01-13 · Final Semester Yale 1995 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1995_p013.jpg|p. 13]], [[attachments/pages/scan_1995_p014.jpg|p. 14]]*

1/13/95 Friday (maybe it was best I didn't go biking.)
Why do I feel so uncomfortable in groups these days? I'm so self-conscious always feeling people ~~[?]~~ guessing at who I am by my appearance - v. conscious that I am white, upper-class and male. Maybe I am too sensitive to these things. I <u>am</u> too sensitive to these things. I don't know if it was better in high-school when I could fit into the athelete, partier, but makes-the-grades crowd. Now I don't feel studly when I go jogging. I feel like just another ex-athelete - and my high-school career was hardly remarkable - balding Yale college kid type. Mountain biking I feel good, but I was scared to go today - scared of getting mugged. There's that place at the end where I saw the stolen bike etc. Climbing I'm still too self conscious on the wall - everyone is looking at me feeling. I feel Daria keeping away from me, that's o.k. I don't think it's good to always be w/ someone, but twice now I've backed out of doing things w/ Sarah & Miranda because I feel ~~[?]~~ they're her territory

**Next page:**

but fuck they've been my friends longer thank you. I feel kind of awkward in friendships like I don't know where they stand; I have to re-ascess them. That is good too I suppose - though you risk feeling lonely falling through the gaps of the instability - is higher highs the trade off? rather than the empty false happiness "hey how are you!" I even reevaluated katherine recently. I came off the incredible high. There's still no doubt I am in love w/ her etc. It's just not tingling these couple of days - she was sad when I talked to her for the first time I've ever known her I think - it's scary how mutual we are: we just can't love each other the same when one of us is down. I think her project might be losing some of its novelty - no - ~~she might have questioned it, or is questioning it, or will.~~ I think any artistic ~~[?]~~ action won't get the attention or excitement it deserves. The art world is very small said Cézanne only a tiny handful can appreciate you. katherine and I see similarly - I think Amy does too but she plays the wiser than thou ~~[?]~~ sage - I completely respect her eye though. I just wait for her to say something. She is so powerful in her silences. I talk too much. In any group there are one or two people who ~~[?]~~ under stand you, maybe - I feel like Susan, Lucia and I were in the same place ~~[?]~~ last night.
"You're weird" Ashley said when I was talking about the attraction of the void. I know she doesn't think I am, but she was saying what most people probably think - what Mora and Campbell think, I think. Beirbaum has a heart I think he was almost crying, choked up last night talking about how much he liked the group. Susan cried one tear when I was telling her how much katherine and I were in love: drawing together etc. Funny, it felt wrote for me. I had already told Ashley and didn't even feel tingly and excited then. Why don't people ever call and invite me places? I guess I make them feel uncomfortable somehow. Maybe I don't react well, I'm not always excited to see / do the same types of things. Even Daria gets tired of trying to loosen me up.

## [[1995-02-21_The-Stamp-on-My-Last-Letter-to-Katherine|The Stamp on My Last Letter to Katherine]]
*1995-02-21 · Final Semester Yale 1995 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1995_p039.jpg|p. 39]]*

2/21 Tues.
I can still taste the stamp I put on my last letter to katherine.
I sat and stared at it against the beige formica of the table in CCL. The image on the stamp glowing. The other two destined to remain un-used until they are lost are still neat & crisp. I just bought them the other day - the first time I made the decision to buy a bunch at once rather than always have to wait in line when I wanted to send her a letter. My life changes too much ever to make plans for the future, Even buying stamps.
The letter sits here before me with ~~[?]~~ memorized address ~~[?]~~ about to embark on a journey w/ the four other letters on their way to her. They're all gonna get there in a bunch. Each one so different from the other, a document of my radically shifting feelings 'till this last one ~~[?] to tell her~~ which contains ~~[?]~~ ~~[?]~~ my breakdown and the subsequent cool acceptance. I do feel comfortable and good. My ego is as strong as hers. She can't get me down. She can't always deny what she let herself feel for me. I don't want <u>her</u> reticent love anymore.

## [[1995-06-15_Letter-Draft-to-Katherine-from-DC|Letter Draft to Katherine, from DC]]
*1995-06-15 (estimated) · DC, Post-College · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1995_p082.jpg|p. 82]], [[attachments/pages/scan_1995_p083.jpg|p. 83]]*

`[the whole draft is struck through with large X's in blue pen, added later; comments in the same blue pen are marked below]`
katherine,
I am at a loss to understand what has happened between us the last couple of months. There is always something which doesn't add up, or makes no sense. When I fell in love with you, you slipped in to warm innumerable ~~[?]~~ spots inside me I didn't know existed, now I am left with holes I don't know how to heal. `[right margin, in blue:]` lame I can only hope that you are happier because of this mess, if not all this would ~~really~~ <u>make no sense at all</u>. There is a retension, repression and forcing in you which I can't understand at all. <u>It doesn't make sense</u>. The signals I recieve, what I feel, and what is going on don't add up.
I have been relatively busy. It turns out that I have quite a few friends living in D.C. this summer so I am rarely at a loss of things to do or people to see. I am trying to find a cheap studio to rent for a few months which would make me very happy - to have ~~[?]~~ my own small private space. I need to paint and build some more sculpture rooms or pieces. For now I am working at the NEA - I just walked in and volunteered. It is quite fun, the people there are wonderfully friendly & amazing, there are quite alot of very `[underlined in blue:]` attractive women who work there too. `[right margin, in blue:]` that's nice I almost got a job at a photo developing lab for professionals where they do all sorts of neat processes including film, but the guy decided that he wanted a full-time

**Next page:**

employee and I want to keep working at the NEA. I `[circled in blue:]` might ~~[?]~~ try some more with a theatrical lighting company - or machine shop to give me sculpture experience but I `[circled in blue:]` might start being a bicycle messenger which is no experience at all but makes <u>tons</u> of money (which is always nice) and would be quite a work out. Any plans beyond that are completely up in the air - though I'm probably going to France in September to try to set up that grant w/ the artisans and then vacation some by myself - I want to go to Florence, and Rome to see museums, and galleries (which aren't open in the summer)
I got your card and the photos which were interesting to see - it's kind of funny how much awkwardness or frustration can be read in our faces. I was scared to death to open your envelope, scared that you would upset the tide of my dialy life, ruin the calm I have been struggling to keep which even if it is not happiness, is more comfortable when it is managable. All I can say is your letter didn't disturb me, it didn't really move me either way, which I thank you for. I'm afraid that this letter though certainly not `[circled in blue, with a question mark:]` a bomb-shell is ~~much~~ less careful. I too am at a loss ~~[?]~~ how to end this letter but ~~think~~ feel it is less condecending just to leave it blank.
Marco
