##### Summary of Pasted Essay (Pages 78–79)
- Surveys monumental sites sited in relation to nature and sky: Celtic stone circles in England and Scotland, Toltec sanctuaries at Monte Albán near Oaxaca, the Parthenon emerging above the Aegean Sea, the Egyptian pyramids, the twin towers of Chartres Cathedral rising above golden wheat fields, Le Nôtre's 17th-century gardens for Louis XIV, and Roman consul Lucio Cornelio Silla's temple to Fortuna Primigenia overlooking Rome.
- Contrasts pre-industrial monuments oriented toward cosmic forces with 19th-century railway stations and 20th-century skyscrapers, leading into American desert works and **James Turrell** as a way to experience the sky directly rather than through memory, imagination, or illusion.

Vertigo = thought you can feel running up behind you, ready to tackle you into the void.
