## [[1994-07-28_Sharing-Paintings-With-Women-and-Fear-of-Men|Sharing Paintings With Women & Fear of Men (Page 85)]]
*1994-07-28 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 85 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_2_p014.jpg|p. 14]]*

What did I accomplish this summer in which I was so excited about my creativity? I let everyone know how excited I was. Katherine is excited to see my paintings. Amy, Daria & Vanessa, Alison — `these are all women. -> all know i've talked about it w/ them all.`

Men make me afraid somehow. I was nervous about Hull's reaction. He connects to all the other men I have known: something about his tobacco chewing, comic book buying & trading music w/ Andrew (always very conscious that Andrew and I where the only men in the class) connects him to Coach Markoff who can look down on everyone b/c they will never be able to be as tough as he is—doing hitting drills w/ no pads, MUP over Roger Staubach. He commands — And something about Papa who is always unapproachable & ready to misunderstand & fly off the handle. The number of times I have felt compassion and even pity for my father who never seems to be able to communicate, are few compared to the times I have been afraid he would start screaming about something irrational about which we could never get him to understand the rational—mom's—explanation.

## [[1994-08-06_Dads-Cards-Katherines-Colors-and-Mountain-Textures|Dad's Cards, Katherine's Colors & Mountain Textures]]
*1994-08-06 · Summer 1994, Travel and Alps · notebook p. 47 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p078.jpg|p. 78]]*

Dad is not too artistic, but he cares so much. His constant cards break my heart when I think about how hard it is to communicate with him and how much he loves us.

Katherine's card puts the reddish (earth) where one would expect the skyline, and the blue sky is reflected in the white earth of the foreground. The textures and details refine the visual impact this card has on me—I wanted to say "beauty," any more, though I don't know what that means. "Visual effect" is more precise, maybe, but does that imply how much I was moved by this image?

My comment about textures rings ever more true now that I have been trying to paint up in the mountains. I have taken many pictures of texture. It is much harder to throw texture in with all the other things I have to think about when making a painting.

## [[2026-11-11_Techy-vs-Arty-Romantic-and-Math-Struggles|Techy vs. Arty Romantic & Family Education Resentment]]
*2026-11-11 · Present, Therapy Notes*

Stereotypes: Hurtful Venn diagram on SNL. Jokes about spies being into tech guys. The drop-off of intimacy with Mi coincides with our return to the US and me taking up tech roles. Why the public allergy to STEM people? I feel this allergy very strongly with my susceptible son who refuses the pursuit of tech/engineering/arch despite being well adapted to this work.
