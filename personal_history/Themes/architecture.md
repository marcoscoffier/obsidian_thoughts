## [[1994-07-21_Singing-in-Woolsey-Rotunda|Singing in Woolsey Rotunda & Acoustic Spaces]]
*1994-07-21 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 67 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p118.jpg|p. 118]], [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p119.jpg|p. 119]]*

I was walking through Woolsey Rotunda on my way home from work, singing as I usually do when I am walking. The sound I was making defined the different types of spaces I was in. It changed drastically from outside with background traffic noises, to the highly resonant rotunda space, and out to Beinecke Plaza which has no traffic noise.

I imagined a space that would try to fool the auditor into believing he was in a massive place by making basically an echo chamber. Just brainstorming: could I get permission to use some of the massive amounts of unused space in Payne Whitney Gymnasium? It would take some finagling—who can I get to help me? How about the building across from Charlie B's? I should ask Ilyon what happened when we got kicked off of that lot, and what the owner said.

I would be excited to work with people who transform run-down areas into playgrounds or sculptures—like the woman whom Katherine helped put poetry on planks and streamers in the woods.
