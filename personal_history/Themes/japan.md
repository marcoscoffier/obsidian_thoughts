**Next page:**

went to Turrell exhibition in Tokyo. not a great way to see his pieces. Japanese are very noisy. Museum experience doesn't work you really need quiet or the bubble is burst - is this a weakness?.
I thought of ~~[?]~~ home or street alterings which one could notice or love even w/ a crowd of people doing thinking other things around you. You have to love something in commotion, in <u>different types of commotion</u> - and they are all different - in order to be happy in the world. The poor person who wanders around looking for places where they can be absolutely alone, quiet to their own thoughts, only to have this peace and stillness shattered by any individual just going about his own business, will be unhappy. - Does this thought doom nature?
1st thoughts of Japan - heated toilet seat w/ water spray and automated lighting system in Miwa's parent's house. And a cement floored cave w/ railings & piped in music w/ titles such as "moon landscape", and "old man's staff" painted on marble plaques.
