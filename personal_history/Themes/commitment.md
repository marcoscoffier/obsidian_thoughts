## [[1994-07-22_Growing-Closer-to-Katherine|Growing Closer to Katherine]]
*1994-07-22 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 74 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p129.jpg|p. 129]]*

I am so happy having talked with Katherine. We are somehow growing closer because we are so far apart. I never felt comfortable calling her my girlfriend or telling her that I loved her until now—it is silly, because we are so far apart, yet we are somehow making a commitment. Our similarities coincide; it's amazing how well and comfortably we not only understand each other, but sometimes go through the same process, having the same intuitions independently.
