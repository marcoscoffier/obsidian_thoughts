## [[1994-06-06_Goodbye-to-Katherine|Katherine Leaves & Emotional Restraint]]
*1994-06-06 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 29 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p048.jpg|p. 48]]*

Well, Katherine left today. I choked as I tried to say "Bye, Babe." I came so close to crying as I walked back to Daria's from the curb, from where Lisa's blue station wagon pulled Katherine's ever-smiling face away from me. I can't remember ever having felt so natural and happy with someone. 

*(Later annotation: That kind of statement is always problematic—I wonder if it will sound like the 10th grader who wrote about Nira to me someday.)*

I always wonder why I never cry. Do I rationalize my way out of it, or am I stunted emotionally? If I am deprived of the crying extreme, I must also be kept from extreme happiness. I know I don't explode into the exuberant, unrestrained laughter that Katherine does. I love that about her; she is often as emotionally free as a 5-year-old, her voice sliding through octaves and registers over which she has as little control as her toddler counterpart.

I want to start to write regularly, so that I may work out the kinks and awkwardnesses of transcribing my thoughts. I want to try to get this process as free as Katherine's laughter, and as that loose painting stroke she so appreciates.

## [[1994-06-15_On-Katherines-Jan-94-Letter-Page-20|On Katherine’s January 1994 Letter (Page 20)]]
*1994-06-15 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 20 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p031.jpg|p. 31]]*

Katherine wrote to me: *(Jan '94)*
> *"One of the things I like most about spending time w/ you is that I don't feel like I have to leave my poetic imagination. I think you meet me w/ your own in a way."*

She believes that the two are separate. *[inserted: That]* Hers is different from everybody else's. I don't. I believe they are different in the specifics, but derive from the same thing. A common, united place. (Her adjectives: *strange, uncertain, moving places [...] in crazy inklings,... the unexplainable.*) I long to find moments when that soul/place/whatever is shared, and thus obviously *[arrow insertion: where I can share this w/ somebody]* would want to maintain a relationship w/ her. I long to partake in / share the experience w/ someone (which can only happen privately) → which is why I like works of art which are still accessible to me (or like the few Diebenkorn abstractions which after a small explanation became accessible to me.)

Katherine, I guess, rejoices in the evidence of someone else's communication w/ the soul/place, but does not need or want to share it. It is personal, and I want to say that she sees the evidence of Jan Groover's *[inserted above: or Diebenkorn's]* communication in their work, and is moved by that rather than by the work itself, but that is no different from what I do, so once again I have come full circle to my belief that this is the same mechanism in all people.

*(Sideways annotation in lower right margin, with arrow pointing to the Jan Groover / Diebenkorn line):*  
**"again this art referance *[sic]* no longer makes any sense to me"**

*1994-07-22 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 74 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p129.jpg|p. 129]]*

I am so happy having talked with Katherine. We are somehow growing closer because we are so far apart. ## [[1994-07-28_Sharing-Paintings-With-Women-and-Fear-of-Men|Sharing Paintings With Women & Fear of Men (Page 85)]]
*1994-07-28 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 85 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_2_p014.jpg|p. 14]]*

What did I accomplish this summer in which I was so excited about my creativity? I let everyone know how excited I was. Katherine is excited to see my paintings. Amy, Daria & Vanessa, Alison — `these are all women. -> all know i've talked about it w/ them all.`

Men make me afraid somehow. I was nervous about Hull's reaction. He connects to all the other men I have known: something about his tobacco chewing, comic book buying & trading music w/ Andrew (always very conscious that Andrew and I where the only men in the class) connects him to Coach Markoff who can look down on everyone b/c they will never be able to be as tough as he is—doing hitting drills w/ no pads, MUP over Roger Staubach. He commands — And something about Papa who is always unapproachable & ready to misunderstand & fly off the handle. The number of times I have felt compassion and even pity for my father who never seems to be able to communicate, are few compared to the times I have been afraid he would start screaming about something irrational about which we could never get him to understand the rational—mom's—explanation.

## [[1994-08-02_Self-Disclosure-and-Marrying-Katherine|Painting vs. Camera, Self-Disclosure & Thinking of Marrying Katherine]]
*1994-08-02 · Summer 1994, Travel and Alps · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p047.jpg|p. 47]], [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p053.jpg|p. 53]]*

Today, taking pictures, I thought it's silly for the painter to fight to represent exactly what a camera sees. A painting is a painting—everyone knows this, so I might as well enjoy it for what it is.

The first 28 pages of this book (not including the notes I have since added) took me a year to write, and I have almost finished the rest in two months. Somehow I am glad that I wrote down this beginning—I write down too much. Maybe it would be more mysterious if I hid these things, like I think I should hide some things in conversation, and not make myself so crystal clear.

*(Later annotation: I still feel these things, probably even stronger—if that is possible, because they weren't feelings that had to do with strength or measurement of any kind; they are good and solid. I've been thinking about living with Katherine—marrying?—a lot lately.)*

## [[1994-08-20_Draft-Letter-to-Katherine|Draft Letter to Katherine: Vocation & Companionship]]
*1994-08-20 · Summer 1994, Travel and Alps · notebook p. 32 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p054.jpg|p. 54]]*

My language is so tense (my handwriting so small). I feel that things changed so much, I'm not sure how to address you. What are you thinking, feeling? What will your first letter to me be like?

I'm feeling very much out on my own—detached from home, and my friends so far, and from you. I'm trying to figure myself out. What do I want to do? Do I gear myself up for some kind of profession? Further studying? A life of academia? What about art? Am I more of an artist at heart? If so, more conceptual? Emotional? A painter? I can't really start planning any life till I know some kind of answer to these questions.

Should I include you in some sort of plan? It seems that if we wanted to see each other for anything more than a short period—which can't be satisfying in the long term, and I feel that is a weak situation for a relationship, though it is well praised by romance novels—on the other hand, I think that I will think of you in many situations in my life even if we are not together.

Which brings me to my next thought: I think that what we began to cultivate (our relationship) was really incredible. I've never felt so natural and empowered. You became a true companion. I'm not trying to say that we should be friends—I loved sharing everything with you: the double sleeping bags and waking up late in the woods, Shalimar, and several nights at Daria's. The essence of our relationship, I think, is a similarity of spirit and thought.
