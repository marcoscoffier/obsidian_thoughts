What did I accomplish this summer in which I was so excited about my creativity? I let everyone know how excited I was. Katherine is excited to see my paintings. Amy, Daria & Vanessa, Alison — `these are all women. -> all know i've talked about it w/ them all.`

Men make me afraid somehow. I was nervous about Hull's reaction. He connects to all the other men I have known: something about his tobacco chewing, comic book buying & trading music w/ Andrew (always very conscious that Andrew and I where the only men in the class) connects him to Coach Markoff who can look down on everyone b/c they will never be able to be as tough as he is—doing hitting drills w/ no pads, MUP over Roger Staubach. ## [[1994-08-10_Swedish-Skiers-and-Gender-Constructs|Swedish Freestyle Skiers & Gender Constructs]]
*1994-08-10 · Summer 1994, Travel and Alps · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p053.jpg|p. 53]], [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p055.jpg|p. 55]]*

I took the Funiculaire down from the Glacier after having hiked up to the Restaurant. I found myself next to two members of the Swedish women's freestyle team—both very feminine and blond. They were talking to a man (from the French freestyle team?) in English. One of them said in broken English, *"When I was little, my mother would get angry because I would always play with the boys, and always had holes in my knees."* Granted, these women were not very big, but I respect how they take pride in what they do, which is a physical pride.

I also read about a 40-year-old mother of a couple kids in Jersey who took up weightlifting; her trainer was an ex-Ms. Olympia or something—very inspiring. They both had very impressive physiques and took pride in how muscular they looked, yet most people would look at them as freaks.

Katherine has ruined so many women for me. By rejecting so many "womanly" things—skirts, etc.—she has made me associate those things with the bad parts of the cultural construct of woman. I have rejected many "macho" aspects of manhood, and remember writing a paper for Lit 300 where I identified myself with the position these feminists were describing. I think that if Katherine and I continue keeping in touch, we will find that we will each swing away from identifying with the opposite sex—she will find comfort with my masculine qualities, as I will find comfort in some feminine qualities she has.

I was thinking of taking pictures of women weightlifters' bodies—beautiful pictures about their muscularity and health, close-ups more personal than a flex pose, to help redefine the image of a woman's body.
