## [[1994-06-20_Katherines-Badlands-Postcard|Katherine's Badlands Postcard]]
*1994-06-20 · Summer 1994, Travel and Alps · scan [[attachments/pages/postcard_1994_katherine_front.jpg|postcard_1994_katherine_front]], [[attachments/pages/postcard_1994_katherine_back.jpg|postcard_1994_katherine_back]]*

`[front: Badlands National Park — a cracked white mud flat in the foreground, green and dry brush in the middle, and a ridge of sharp orange-red peaks against a pale blue sky]`

`[back, printed:]` BADLANDS NATIONAL PARK. #14727 G-2/17 Mud cracks in a dry wash illustrate the semiarid climate and highly erosive nature of Badlands rocks—two factors that help to create the spectacular Badlands scenery. Photographer—Carr Clifton
`[postmark: Seattle WA, JUN 1994; 19¢ hot-air-balloon stamp]`
`[address:]` Marco Scoffier / PO Box 4191 Yale Station / New Haven, CT 06520 / - AIR MAIL -

June 20 - 1AM Seattle WA youth hostel
Dear Marco, Lisa & I had our first shower since Minnesota & finally brushed our teeth. Went crazy sleeping at rest stops in the car for 3 nights in a row across the Dakota's, Wyoming, & Montana. Thought you'd appreciate this postcard. <u>oh!</u>, the land out west is just waiting for you, Marco!
2 things: make sure you catch the next Letterman Show w/ the 2 Pakistani men at Mt. Rushmore. Lisa & I were there which made the tourist attraction all the more exciting!
also, walking thru the gay district, capitol hill, in Seattle, tonite saw your fellow-SOB, Tristan sitting at an outside cafe. He definitely saw me & then pretended not to when I walked by. He probably thought Lisa was my lover and that I was being mean as usual. Anyhow we didn't talk. I don't think we've officially met. (to you)
Hope all's well. Thought of you & skiing when I ran down a steep path on a mountain in Missoula, Montana. Worked my legs hard & today they hurt. Love you, Katherine
(should be home by this Wed.)
`[beside the printed "Interior, SD 57750":]` (South Dakota)

> [!note]- Transcription notes
> A postcard from Katherine, kept in the 1994 notebook. It is the card Marco describes on 6 August 1994 in [[1994-08-06_Dads-Cards-Katherines-Colors-and-Mountain-Textures]]: "the reddish (earth) where one would expect the skyline, and the blue sky is reflected in the white earth of the foreground." The year comes from the Seattle postmark (June 1994). "SOB" is probably the Yale Society of Orpheus and Bacchus. The era follows the notebook it was kept in.

Hongkong very clean very modern - I unfortunately didn't eat very well nor did I get the picture of the shipping docks w/ all of HK's exports, which you can see best from the over pass on the way to the airport
`[in blue:]` The senses full - Agus w/ both TV & Radio on at full blast. ~~[?]~~ Mid day super sugar 4 preadolescent girls doing doing-bop video w/ every video effect, imaginable
Insane Japanese television - w/ stars appearing on contest shows and ex-famous athletes who are now comedians preparing & to participate in an aerobics championship semi-real semi-planned `[in the margin:]` televised pool tournament between music stars.
Screech Parlor of Japanese women singers
Japanese TV is very funny - and the Japanese are very friendly similar - freek show Barbie boy surgery & the S. American mini-dwarf

**Next page:**

went to Turrell exhibition in Tokyo. not a great way to see his pieces. Japanese are very noisy. Museum experience doesn't work you really need quiet or the bubble is burst - is this a weakness?.
I thought of ~~[?]~~ home or street alterings which one could notice or love even w/ a crowd of people doing thinking other things around you. You have to love something in commotion, in <u>different types of commotion</u> - and they are all different - in order to be happy in the world. The poor person who wanders around looking for places where they can be absolutely alone, quiet to their own thoughts, only to have this peace and stillness shattered by any individual just going about his own business, will be unhappy. - Does this thought doom nature?
1st thoughts of Japan - heated toilet seat w/ water spray and automated lighting system in Miwa's parent's house. And a cement floored cave w/ railings & piped in music w/ titles such as "moon landscape", and "old man's staff" painted on marble plaques.

> Undated; a trip through Hong Kong to Japan, the first in this notebook. The Hong Kong paragraph is in black, the rest in blue. Agus is not identified. Miwa's parents' house is mentioned again on the next pages. Turrell had several exhibitions in Tokyo in the 1990s; which one this was isn't said.
