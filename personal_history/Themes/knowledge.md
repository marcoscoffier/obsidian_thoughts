## [[1994-07-28_Hollander-Bloom-Ashbery-and-Yales-Library|Writing Style, Hollander/Ashbery Paper & the Yale Library Stacks]]
*1994-07-28 · Yale, New Haven · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_2_p013.jpg|p. 13]]*

on writing style w/ me this summer rather than all this art theory that I'm going to be incapable of doing anything w/ b/c I can't write well—I'm always sloppy & have trouble verbalising my thoughts. It feels better to have gotten that down.

I wish I could have found out what Hollander thought of my paper. I felt that for the first time I had brought something out of a text which I had not been led to by hand. The part about Merill's [above: *Merrill's*] poem was all Hollander & Bloom: Merill [above: *Merrill*] is the poet's poet, I am not a poet. The part about Ashbery was mine. It somehow combined everything I had been thinking about in the studio & in sculpture class but was unable to turn into artworks. Ashbery was talking my language. I knew from where the metonomies for his metaphors came (I'll probably be throwing these terms around based on the cursory understanding I got out of Eco's essay. For a while)

I thought a lot this year. Too much really; I didn't do. I have back-tracked, realizing that it is too ambitious for me to want to make paintings about stereoptics or 180° vision at this point in my development. I have to learn about paint, about thinking, seeing & loving in a painterly way, which doesn't necessarily imply becoming stupid about other things. Frank Auerbach said all of the Greatest Painters (why caps?) he can think of read a lot. It is impossible to say anything worthwhile without reading. Joyce was an insatiable reader.

My anxiety has cleared. I know that I love expanding my network of fixed (appropriated) points in the ocean of knowledge (WW) that makes up the stacks of books in the Yale Library system and in the Library system of the world. What does a library contain? Information? Knowledge? Art? Life? Humanity? Reproductions of all the above? traces of all the above? Documentation of...?
