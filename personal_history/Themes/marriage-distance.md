## [[1995-02-21_The-Stamp-on-My-Last-Letter-to-Katherine|The Stamp on My Last Letter to Katherine]]
*1995-02-21 · Final Semester Yale 1995 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1995_p039.jpg|p. 39]]*

2/21 Tues.
I can still taste the stamp I put on my last letter to katherine.
I sat and stared at it against the beige formica of the table in CCL. The image on the stamp glowing. The other two destined to remain un-used until they are lost are still neat & crisp. I just bought them the other day - the first time I made the decision to buy a bunch at once rather than always have to wait in line when I wanted to send her a letter. My life changes too much ever to make plans for the future, Even buying stamps.
The letter sits here before me with ~~[?]~~ memorized address ~~[?]~~ about to embark on a journey w/ the four other letters on their way to her. They're all gonna get there in a bunch. Each one so different from the other, a document of my radically shifting feelings 'till this last one ~~[?] to tell her~~ which contains ~~[?]~~ ~~[?]~~ my breakdown and the subsequent cool acceptance. I do feel comfortable and good. My ego is as strong as hers. She can't get me down. She can't always deny what she let herself feel for me. I don't want <u>her</u> reticent love anymore.
`[at the foot of the page: two unused 40¢ US Airmail stamps, "William T. Piper, Aviation Pioneer", pasted in]`

> [!note]- Transcription notes
> The two stamps pasted at the bottom of the page are probably "the other two destined to remain un-used". CCL is Yale's Cross Campus Library. 21 February 1995 was a Tuesday.

## [[2026-11-11_Techy-vs-Arty-Romantic-and-Math-Struggles|Techy vs. Arty Romantic & Family Education Resentment]]
*2026-11-11 · Present, Therapy Notes*

Stereotypes: Hurtful Venn diagram on SNL. Jokes about spies being into tech guys. The drop-off of intimacy with Mi coincides with our return to the US and me taking up tech roles. Why the public allergy to STEM people? I feel this allergy very strongly with my susceptible son who refuses the pursuit of tech/engineering/arch despite being well adapted to this work. I mentioned how tacky I found Jensen’s house. I can only appreciate a part of each of my overlapping worlds.

I wish I had been supported in my early struggles with math and physics at university. My mother was happy to push us into completely non-survivable majors with only the illusion of financial backing—the illusion would vaporize as soon as it was needed. I sort of survived, by fighting and re-organizing my life. Off to a late start, constant catch-up and workarounds. My brothers fared much worse. The albatross of Papa's Polytechnique lesson books. Anger that I didn’t have the support to push and do the hard things.

## [[2026-11-11_Yale-Meat-Market-and-Cibone-Show|The Yale Meat Market & Design Show at Cibone]]
*2026-11-11 · Present, Therapy Notes*

I think what "grosses me out" about the girls who would flock to preppy Yale singers is that they were deluded by an image, a dream or hope, without knowing the person inside. The worst guys would take advantage of the power imbalance to debase and abuse these confused girls. The whole meat market grossed me out. I felt powerless to change it and I didn’t want to be involved. And yet still today I am thrown into the Yale preppy stereotype. Bret Kavanaugh and Chris Cuomo are despicable yet hold highly visible positions of power and influence. I have shame for those times I might have exerted a power.

I went to a design show at Cibone with Miwa—so much posturing and posing. No interesting conversations. I feel so much distance to the fashionable. I can’t feel excited by the moods or the expensive objects—even the carefully handmade ones. Not in that vibe. I always feel so looked at, so judged. I make the wrong laughable choice. All this style is such a fad.

We had dinner alone with Miwa afterwards. She said she was exhausted by all the social tension. I felt a bit closer to her, but with all the roiling tension I have inside, still very far.
