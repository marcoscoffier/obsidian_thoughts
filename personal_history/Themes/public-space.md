\- Robinson Crusoe

<u>Public space</u> - always planned, by someone → unfortunately too often ~~[?]~~ the planners aren't invested, they don't live there - don't even go there
many forgotten spaces that individuals don't have the means (financial, physical, mechanised building etc.) to transform.
How could ~~[?] [?] [?]~~
public space actually become public → not planned for the public — but created, mutated by ~~them~~. it

<u>Playground Arch.</u>: There was an article in the Smithsonian mag about child psych. architects who observe playground behaviour. Most used & essent element → moveable, transformable stuff, sticks, <u>SAND</u>, dirt etc. The planned stuff →steering wheels etc. ~~[?]~~ go virtually un-used except when its function is completely deturned (new thing to hang from, alien base etc.)

<u>Traditional cuisine</u>: Nutritionists find that a french balance of high-fat & tannin in red wine actually fights off heart disease → similar wonders in Eastern trad. cuisines. The human body naturally automatically → certainly un-logically - knows what is best for it.

I have a deep conviction in the ingenuity of the individual human being; a deep regret for any system which hampers this.
