## [[1994-06-09_Retrospective-Annotation-on-Letter-to-Edo|Retrospective Annotation on Letter to Edo (June 9, 1999)]]
*1994-06-09 · Yale, New Haven · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p030.jpg|p. 30]]*

This page has become really problematic. There are very few pages in this book where I really have no idea what I was thinking about, but this is one. The throes of passion or pain had thrown me to rather illogical conclusions.

After having thought beyond the Kant/Schiller universals - I realize the importance of seeing these things as specific to individuals—you respect that person more by trying to discover their particular experience, than by simply assuming theirs is the same as yours. The mechanisms may be the same, we are all humans, but all with very different experiences; those that mean the most are the most personal. *(See page 52 about my change of heart over Twombly and Pollock—coming to understand what they are doing and grow to repsect and admire it even if it is originally very alien. ## [[1994-06-15_On-Katherines-Jan-94-Letter-Page-20|On Katherine’s January 1994 Letter (Page 20)]]
*1994-06-15 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 20 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p031.jpg|p. 31]]*

Katherine wrote to me: *(Jan '94)*
> *"One of the things I like most about spending time w/ you is that I don't feel like I have to leave my poetic imagination. I think you meet me w/ your own in a way."*

She believes that the two are separate. *[inserted: That]* Hers is different from everybody else's. I don't. I believe they are different in the specifics, but derive from the same thing. A common, united place. (Her adjectives: *strange, uncertain, moving places [...] in crazy inklings,... the unexplainable.*) I long to find moments when that soul/place/whatever is shared, and thus obviously *[arrow insertion: where I can share this w/ somebody]* would want to maintain a relationship w/ her. I long to partake in / share the experience w/ someone (which can only happen privately) → which is why I like works of art which are still accessible to me (or like the few Diebenkorn abstractions which after a small explanation became accessible to me.)

Katherine, I guess, rejoices in the evidence of someone else's communication w/ the soul/place, but does not need or want to share it. It is personal, and I want to say that she sees the evidence of Jan Groover's *[inserted above: or Diebenkorn's]* communication in their work, and is moved by that rather than by the work itself, but that is no different from what I do, so once again I have come full circle to my belief that this is the same mechanism in all people.

*(Sideways annotation in lower right margin, with arrow pointing to the Jan Groover / Diebenkorn line):*  
**"again this art referance *[sic]* no longer makes any sense to me"**
