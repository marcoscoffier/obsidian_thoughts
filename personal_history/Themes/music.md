## [[1994-07-15_The-Chopin-Nocturne-Room|Installation Idea: The Chopin Nocturne Room]]
*1994-07-15 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 65 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p110.jpg|p. 110]], [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p112.jpg|p. 112]]*

My next idea was to create a piece for the Chopin Nocturne:
* **Entrance:** You enter a room and have to go around a wall upon which I have written a poem about my experience of going to sleep with this music—my mother would play it to calm me down when I was a baby.
* **Architecture:** A ramp leads you down into a dark room with a low ceiling and mattresses or cushions on the ground.
* **Lighting & Sound:** Venetian blinds with daylight-like lights behind them to simulate windows so the light disperses in stripes, lights diffusing gently along the walls, and hidden speakers.
* **Threshold:** A secret passageway—something you could pass right by without noticing, with no door, just a subtle light above a doorway. It doesn't scream *ENTRANCE*, just a place you notice and sneak into to find magic: *"Am I supposed to be coming back here? Is this off-limits?"* Inside, an unobstructed lifeline back out which you choose not to take.

## [[1994-07-21_Problem-with-Pre-Recorded-Music-in-Art|The Problem with Pre-Recorded Music in Art]]
*1994-07-21 · Yale, New Haven · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p119.jpg|p. 119]]*

The problem with using music in an installation is that it connotes so many other things: the instruments, the time when the composer lived, previous associations about this music by the listener, the performance, the recording, the stereo. Should I write the sound myself, or look at some modern composers who are obscure enough as not to have created a reference frame?

## [[1994-07-27_Dialogues-with-Nature-Smoke-Fly-and-Squirrels|Dialogues with Nature: Smoke, Fly & Squirrels (7/27)]]
*1994-07-27 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 80 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_2_p004.jpg|p. 4]], [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_2_p005.jpg|p. 5]]*

**7/27** — I remember several dialogues with nature:

I was lying on my back in the Saybrook grass court yard listening to Dvořák's cello concerto. There was a flume of smoke rising from the dark roof, illuminated by the moon which was full, and close behind it. The plume seemed to dance as it rose. It would go from heavy to almost non-existent. The Music was happening inside my head between the two earpieces of my headphones & the plume was outside in the cool night. There were times when the plume seemed to react to the music I was listening to; almost vanish with a decrescendo or dance wildly in a more vigorous part. Those moments of syncrony were wonderful and random, and wonderful for being random.

I had the same sort of feeling watching a fly in my room. For some reason flies circle around in the very center of the room where a string hangs down from the ceiling light. Its motions were generally much quicker than the smoke's had been, but it too seemed to get some kind of rhythm or pattern from the music.

I will never forget a wonderful day at the end of exams when I went for a long run & lay down under a chestnut tree near the Divinity school. Two squirrels were playing or flirting near me & at times it seemed I understood what they were communicating to each other and they felt my presence in their world.

I think it is interesting to think of these interior/exterior running dialogues. When are they synchronized? Can they be synchronized? or are we just experiencing random connection patterns which happen to seem similar?

## [[1994-12-28_Flavors-of-Life-and-Katherine|Flavors of Life and Katherine]]
*1994-12-28 · Final Semester Yale 1995 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1995_p002.jpg|p. 2]]*

12/28/94
I love how life has so many different flavors. I never could have imagned sensations like I had on the trip visiting katherine or like I just had listening to Dvorak's Requiem. I find the flavors come in books and music or paintings or people each of those four is a means of communicating the same type of disarming molding and manipulation of your soul. You have to let your self be openned up - splayed like I felt on my bed listening to Dvorak after the exhausting emotional turmoils of not going w/ the SOB's on mario's insane amounts of driving tour and the complicated manneuvering of our lives katherine & I are going to under take for the next couple years 'till we can both settle down to something, hopefully together. I think that we are both people who will continue to change for our entire lives - I don't think we will ever be content in any kind of stasis. It makes me love katherine so much when she couldn't just waste time in Berkeley to be with me. We are happiest with each other and ourselves when we are forging ahead. I hope we can find a situation when we can forge ahead together - where our goal might be common - that could be for in the future or never. will we ever have found ourselves enough to unite in that way? Is such a union possible or feasable or ~~wanted~~ to be desired?.
We share something I don't know if it can be called love It certainly is closer to love than anything else, but so unusual, different and personal - a feeling which could only happen between us two that I hesitate to classify it with the generic hackneyed name, 'love'.
I'm very excited to write in this beautiful book with the hole in the first page.
