## [[1994-06-19_Camping-Dream-and-Composite-Collage|Camping Dream & Composite Collage]]
*1994-06-19 · Yale, New Haven · notebook p. 39 · scan [[attachments/pages/scan_1994_1_p064.jpg|p. 64]]*

I have to write down the thoughts I had today in the car—I was very excited about associations in visual art. Like the exercise Prof. Hull gave us where we had to pick a favorite painting and then setup a still life with similar compositional elements or one which emphasized those elements we liked the most. I couldn't fall in love with a painting whose specific composition I liked, so I picked a small statue of a yawning boy. I then made a still life whcih imitated the main curves of his body. I knew I wasn't exactly fulfilling the assignment but I remember being extremely excited about what I was doing. 

-- Also, recently I had a dream while camping with Katherine. I don't remember it completely now, but at the important part some art works and professors (I know Hull was one) were walking by my work. They were shocked by it. I had done things I didn't remember making—I know it was mine, yet I hadn't realized it.

It was a collage—many composite images which had a unified compositional whole, but the image would completely change depending on how far away I was. Every time I looked at it, it was a different image, and yet I remember being amazed that it was all one image—it even changed sizes all the time.
