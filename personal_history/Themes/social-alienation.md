## [[2026-11-11_Yale-Meat-Market-and-Cibone-Show|The Yale Meat Market & Design Show at Cibone]]
*2026-11-11 · Present, Therapy Notes*

I think what "grosses me out" about the girls who would flock to preppy Yale singers is that they were deluded by an image, a dream or hope, without knowing the person inside. The worst guys would take advantage of the power imbalance to debase and abuse these confused girls. The whole meat market grossed me out. I felt powerless to change it and I didn’t want to be involved. And yet still today I am thrown into the Yale preppy stereotype. Bret Kavanaugh and Chris Cuomo are despicable yet hold highly visible positions of power and influence. I have shame for those times I might have exerted a power.

I went to a design show at Cibone with Miwa—so much posturing and posing. No interesting conversations. I feel so much distance to the fashionable. I can’t feel excited by the moods or the expensive objects—even the carefully handmade ones. Not in that vibe. I always feel so looked at, so judged. I make the wrong laughable choice. All this style is such a fad.

We had dinner alone with Miwa afterwards. She said she was exhausted by all the social tension. I felt a bit closer to her, but with all the roiling tension I have inside, still very far.
