> Undated, over six pages. Notes from a meeting or panel on the National Endowment for the Arts' visual-arts programme, with Marco's own thoughts mixed in (the opening paragraphs, "I think that art thinking is in everyone", "can I work on this"). The talk of an amendment to cancel individual fellowships "except Literature", and of the fiscal year starting Oct. 1, fits the 1995 Congressional fight over NEA funding, when grants to individual visual artists were cut; so this is probably summer 1995, perhaps while Marco was working in Washington DC (see the DC address on [[1994-12-28_Inside-Cover-Addresses-and-Contacts]]). Ned Cohn is not identified; "the Pavillion" is probably the Old Post Office Pavilion in Washington, where the NEA had its offices.
