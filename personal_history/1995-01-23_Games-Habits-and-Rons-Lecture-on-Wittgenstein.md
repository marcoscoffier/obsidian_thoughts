# Games, Habits and Ron's Lecture on Wittgenstein

1/23 the non-essentialist has just expanded the idea of what can be art, what can hold the spark that some have called 'beauty' and we now call 'art'. If anyone makes a statement 'that is art or good art' or 'that is not art or bad art' he is making an essentialist's decision: An object has, or does not have a certain quality which he has called art.
I am very afraid of how attractive I find my reading about Wittgenstein's Aesthetics - 'game' = 'art' - or I don't know if he has come to that yet all the 'concepts' which make up a game are perenially flexible. I am reading this after my OX fun revelation we always play games, I was thinking of the joy of seeing them again at how I wanted to give Campbell and Prog hugs worth of the ones they have given me - not to mention others - Lucia has caught the same bug as me whereas Ashley, Campbell Prog, & Beirbaum already have it others are in between. It is strange to think the same thing happened to Erik last year.
How does one reconcile the game w/ your work? Can they be the same?
