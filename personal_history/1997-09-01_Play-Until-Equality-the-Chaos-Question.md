# Play Until Equality: the Chaos Question

play as many games as it takes to have equality which ends the match
`[sketch: two diagrams of arrows bouncing back and forth from a line labelled "start/stop", the second between "start/stop" and "outer limit"]`
\- get to <u>equality</u> is the goal.
\_\_\_
\- is it possible that this goes on forever?
can have situation controlled parameters (temp.) → Chaos question
12/0 is a much greater differance than 180/88 or 1000/988 ... the chances of 1000 → 0 are v. small.
`[sketch: a branching tree of arrows spreading out from a point labelled "st/stop", some branches marked "stop", one "continue"; labelled "↳ odds are to return to center"]`
\- odds are to return to center for each bounce so as the ball gets away from the center the odds for returning the system's center are less and less
\_\_\_
how to track the escapees?
