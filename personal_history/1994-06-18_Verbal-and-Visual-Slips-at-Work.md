# Verbal & Visual Slips at Work

Today, just now, I wrote "YS" when I meant "Y3"—visually the S and the 3 flipped around on me. Earlier I saw Alison Henyey on the street and was telling her about these slips of mine when I am tired at work. She is responsible for a bunch of Korean kids this summer and was talking about how they can't say "L." When another Romanian kid asked her how to spell her name, she responded: "A-R-I-S-O-N."

I also switched "FEV" and "10" on Katherine's zip code because I misheard, and mixed up "quite" for "quiet." Even when reading and typing call numbers silently, I must have made an association by sound—there is always a time when you write or type when you can hear your own voice in your head even though you aren't speaking.
