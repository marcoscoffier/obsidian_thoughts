# "Hell Is Eternal": Ratzinger and Hofstadter

"Hell is eternal" quote in NYT mag by Ratzinger ~~[?]~~ and thinking about last week Hofstater's the soul is a pattern imprinted in the connections & chemistry of the brain You can be dearly remembered or bitterly hated = heaven or hell
\- 2nd thought how deep & how confusing these abstractions which our brain readily accepts. A world w/out metaphor has less risk of confusion, ~~[?]~~ so what is gained by metaphor? why evolution of metaphor?
