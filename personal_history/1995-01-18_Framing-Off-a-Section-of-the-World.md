# Framing Off a Section of the World

~~[?]~~ 1/18 Framing off a section of ~~[?]~~ the world. Isolating it, diluting it. ~~[?]~~ Perhaps I can arrive at creating the mental image. Recreating the visuals I had in the dream about falling.

**Next page:**

\- Garth and Graham both said that upon first meeting me I did not seem to be the most friendly person. They were both put off by me really. I feel people stay away from me, you know. I feel that they find me off putting. I am <u>not</u> always ready to be sociable.
The realization that I am here - right now. In the L&B room, at Yale, during the day a day... That is the feeling I want to get w/ the pieces, like I got w/ Turrell @ MOMA.
