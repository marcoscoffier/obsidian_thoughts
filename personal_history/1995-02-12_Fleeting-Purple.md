# Fleeting Purple

2/12 I am really not being good about recording my thoughts - they are going too quickly. I Got alot of inspiration from my other notebooks on Sat. reminded of all the profitable thought I had which I can use. It would be nice to have as much profitable work. Easier said than done - right?
It is nice to be able to see some of my dreamings, there are many more steps to actually building the ideas, no matter how much you try to predict before you have made it.
Wonderful sight yesterday: I was walking by the new Henry Luce Hall - they had an orange plastic construction fence up. I have been enjoying the snow's beautiful blue shadows - and through the orange fence It looked purple! The part of the fence in the sun - made the purple. The part in shadow the snow looked its regular blue. the blue on the ~~Fence~~ must have mixed out any effect.
This moment outside of me - can I make it for somebody else? drag Ron outside.
\- not something to make a painting about
