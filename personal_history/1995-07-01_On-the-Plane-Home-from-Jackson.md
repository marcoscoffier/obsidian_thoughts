# On the Plane Home from Jackson

On the plane home from Jackson
I want to
fall through the thick coils of air
wipping around my flesh
tugging at my hairs
to land in the soft white silence
of a cloud.

There I could
tumble and scramble
through its billowy whiteness
climbing my way through
the bottomless formlessness
In sun and shadow, wind and silence
Alone, tired and happy.
