# Five Frames: Taped-In Slips

`[a page made of slips of paper taped in; read left to right, top to bottom]`
`[slip, top left:]`
John Cage marvelled that the finest trained musicians would balk when he gave them ~~[?]~~ freedom with the score, when he released art making into their hands. When his part in the artistic process was reduced to siging his name and thus framing the experience.
~~He was~~
My piece can at best bring attention to what was already there. I have made nothing but five frames.
How can I suck the viewer in?
`[slip, top right:]`
The power of poetry is the power of life.
Art makes my heart beat.
It is hard to compare any companion to this relationship.
\_\_\_
The grass shoot is never far from a companion.
Why must the human when he feels himself so full of life and growth Sprout so alone?
`[torn spiral-notebook slip, lower right:]`
The little details are keeping me from making magic.
They are annoying but they make the barely noticable but essential differenke that Robert Wilson's friend felt after Wilson had stayed in his house & re-arranged the objects.
`[typed slip, lower left:]`
We
open our eyes and ears seeing life
each day excellent as it is
This realization no longer needs art.
\--John Cage
`[typed slip, at the foot:]`
I believe any sensible person would change his pictures, however good, for windows.
\--John Ruskin

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated. The two typed quotations were also copied by hand in [[1995-03-11_A-Map-of-New-Haven-for-the-Thesis-Show]]. Printed text showing through one slip from the back reads, in mirror image, "Sun. April 2 / Mon. April 3 / Tues. April 4", which fits 1995 and suggests the page was made up in early April. The "five frames" may be the five pieces of the thesis show.
