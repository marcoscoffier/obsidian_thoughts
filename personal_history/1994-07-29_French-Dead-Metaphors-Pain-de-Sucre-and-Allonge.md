# French Dead Metaphors: Un Pain de Sucre & Un Weekend Allongé (Page 89)

I was thinking about French metaphors (or dead metaphors really) today in the car listening to my dad speak.

At breakfast before leaving: *Un pain de sucre*—a bread of sugar makes absolutely no sense in english but it is the dead figure in French for a sugar cube.

*Un weekend allongé*: My first thought was to a lying down weekend which is the primary meaning (or at least the meaning I first equate to *allonger*) but then I thought of an extended weekend. Extend can also mean to lie down in the reflexive to extend one's self or body but this is a secondary meaning. A dictionary would definitely help this thought.
