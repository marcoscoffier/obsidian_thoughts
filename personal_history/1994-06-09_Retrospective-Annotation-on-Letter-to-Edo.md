# Retrospective Annotation on Letter to Edo (June 9, 1999)

This page has become really problematic. There are very few pages in this book where I really have no idea what I was thinking about, but this is one. The throes of passion or pain had thrown me to rather illogical conclusions.

After having thought beyond the Kant/Schiller universals - I realize the importance of seeing these things as specific to individuals—you respect that person more by trying to discover their particular experience, than by simply assuming theirs is the same as yours. The mechanisms may be the same, we are all humans, but all with very different experiences; those that mean the most are the most personal. *(See page 52 about my change of heart over Twombly and Pollock—coming to understand what they are doing and grow to repsect and admire it even if it is originally very alien.
