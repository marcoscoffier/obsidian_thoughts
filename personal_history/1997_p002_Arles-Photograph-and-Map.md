# Arles: Photograph and Map

**Page 2:** A colour photograph pasted on the right half of the page: a close-up of someone's neck and open shirt collar, with the word `ARLES` written on the skin of the neck; a finger points to it. Around it, light pencil sketches: a winding line like a road or river, small buildings, a cylinder, and at the bottom a street with rows of windows. One tiny label near the middle: `[Peretures[?] assés[?] 4]`.

**Page 3:** A pencil map of Arles, with a route drawn in pen down the left side.

* **Pen route (top to bottom):** `la mer` → `Arles` → `[sketch: a heart]` `paris` → `Caen[?] Lyon` → `Normandi`
* **Pencil map:** `Gare monoprix` along a railway line at the top; `la Rhone` curving down the page; `ARENES` (an oval); `Amphitheatre`; a square marked `place` with `café café café café` around it; `TOMBES`; a dot with `You are here!`

> [!note] Transcription notes
> The photograph and the map seem to belong together (Arles written on the neck, and the map of Arles on the facing page). "Caen" is uncertain; the word could be "Cagen". The faint writing showing through page 3 is from the next page.
