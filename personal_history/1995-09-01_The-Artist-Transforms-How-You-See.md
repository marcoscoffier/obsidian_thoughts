# The Artist Transforms How You See

`[three cards taped onto the page, written in blue ink:]`
`[card 1:]`
~~The~~ The artist or art is the person or thing which transforms how you see your everyday common place world. Schlovsky's defn. is to literal not a poem which uses unusual ~~words~~ metaphors. Reading his poem will not make you think tiger every time you see a couch or whatever
`[card 2:]`
If I walk down the street, the same street I walk down every day to my home and it seems some how wonderful to me that is the presence of art - be it the left over glow of the woman I kissed the night before or just the sun casting a unique hue, you cannot separate what is going on outside
`[card 3:]`
from inside those two not completely independant systems are what produce what we know as life.
Art ~~[?]~~ plays w/ these two it affects the inner system through the same channels that the outside world can, ~~and yet~~ but is furthermore created and thus contains the wonder of communication.
`[on the page, to the right of the first card, in black:]`
The astronomer is a great looker or observer can the definition of artist be the one who teaches ~~[?]~~ you to see - helps you see.

> [!note] Transcription notes
> Undated. "Schlovsky" is Viktor Shklovsky, whose essay "Art as Technique" (1917) defines art as defamiliarisation (*ostranenie*); see "Defamiliarization" in [[1995-06-01_Art-Is-Everywhere-Romance-with-the-Viewer]]. The cards are water-stained.
