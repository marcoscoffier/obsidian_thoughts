# Audience and Creator: Two Realms

`[two more cards taped onto the page, in blue ink, continuing the cards on the facing page:]`
`[sketch: two overlapping circles. The left one is labelled "outside", with a small circle in its centre labelled "inside" and an arrow from "audience" below. The right one has a small circle in its centre with an arrow from "~~artist~~ creator (not necess. a person)". The shaded overlap is labelled "~~art~~ creation (not necessarily art)".]`
~~These are~~ the two realms where we can exist one can either strive to become the MOST receptive & thus the best audience member which is a wonderful place to be (Zen monk etc.) or to be a creator - one who strives to affect ~~the~~ other's inner world.
