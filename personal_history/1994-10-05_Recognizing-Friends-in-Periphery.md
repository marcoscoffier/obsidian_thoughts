# Recognizing Friends in Periphery & Katherine's Likeness

Recognized Paul flying by on my bike—I didn't even look at him, yet something out of the corner of my eye registered as Paul. Similarly, none of the paintings or drawings I have done of Katherine are detailed around the face, yet they strike me as a likeness—it is something about body position, the way she stands, internal proportions creating a feeling of gravity.
