# Painting Without Boundaries, Narrow Corridors & 3D Lightbulb Projection (Page 95)

It is impossible to paint a picture w/out boundaries. The impact of an image is all about a gestalt whole — all at once effect. Change one part of the sea, you affect every other part. Is it possible to make a painting w/out boundaries?

A corridor, too narrow to escape the image. `<- paintings you have to walk into, 3D. they create a space between them.`

> *[Diagram 1: Two large parallel canvas panels forming a narrow corridor one walks between.]*

`<- paint a whole room?`

> *[Diagram 2: Isometric cut-away of a four-walled painted cityscape interior.]*

- paint on a sphere which is transparent in 6 point perspective & put a light inside
- paint on a light bulb? it would reflect the painting on all the walls. *[Sketch of painted lightbulb:]* **3D slide projector.**
