# A Dictionary Is Amazing

A dictionary is amazing - that its truths don't exsist except that people have already agreed upon them. A writer, which are the core examples & guides of these french dictionaries - knows what words or expressions people know, everyone knows and uses them to their fullest to make them aware of what they don't know.
