> ### Notes
>
> - The original file names were `Papa_Ferry_<n>.jpg`. The order was checked against the scanner's timestamps and kept: the new numbers run from 01 to 38.
> - The slides fall into three groups: the car ferry (01–10), Cappadocia (11–30: rock-cut churches, cave dwellings, fairy chimneys, a village below a cliff) and an autumn walk in a mountain gorge with a tea-house stop and a small waterfall (31–38). The setting of the last group looks like the Alborz above Tehran, but that is a guess; nothing is written on the slides.
> - The ferry port is not identified; slide 07 looks like Corfu (?). The people in the slides are not named here. The Citroën DS and the Beetle on the quay are the same car type as in the 1959 and 1961 trips.
> - Slides 01–03 are three takes of the same scene; 01 has a pink light leak. Slides 21–22, 24–26 and 34–35 are pairs or series of the same view.
