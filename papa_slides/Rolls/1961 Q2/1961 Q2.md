> ### The Karaj dam (Amir Kabir dam)
>
> - **Dates**: Built from 1957 to 1961 (1336 to 1340 on the Iranian calendar) and opened in 1961. It is described as the first multi-purpose dam in Iran, a concrete arch dam supplying Tehran's drinking water and irrigation. Early studies go back to the 1930s and 1940s. One Persian source gives a slightly later range of about 1958 to 1963.
> - **Builder**: Sources disagree: one says the American firm Morrison-Knudsen (1957 to 1961), another a French company. This is unresolved.
> - **Consistency with these slides**: Photos 21 and 22 were taken in spring 1961. The dam wall still carries scaffolding while the reservoir already holds water, which fits a dam being finished and filled in 1961. The water-skiing in the Super 8 films ("Ski Nautique Karadj, Été 63", "Juillet 65 Ski Nautique") is after the 1961 opening, so the lake existed by then.
> - **Other dams ruled out**: Latyan dam (built about 1963 to 1967) and Lar dam (about 1974 to 1981) are too late for a spring 1961 photo.
> - **Moreau's site**: A separate folder, "Chantier Moreau" (Nov–Dec 1961), falls after the opening. It could be final works at the Karaj dam, or another Moreau site. To be checked against those photos.
> - **Sources**: Dates come from web sources that do not fully agree; they are worth confirming against the Iranica entry.

> ### Abali ski area (Abe Ali)
>
> - **Where**: Abali is a ski area north-east of Tehran on the Haraz road (the road toward Amol), roughly 55 to 75 km from the capital according to the sources. Damavand is visible from it, as in slide 07.
> - **History**: Several sources say it was the first ski slope in Iran to get mechanical lifts, in 1953, and call it the oldest ski area in Iran or near Tehran. One Persian source says a few skiers trained there around 1956 for the Winter Olympics. No primary source gives the opening date.
> - **Fit with the slides**: Spring 1961 is after the 1953 lifts, so Abali was already an established slope. That fits the box line "Fin de ski a Abe Ali".
> - **Sources**: [Abali Ski Resort (Surfiran)](https://surfiran.com/abali-ski-resort/), [Abali Ski Resort (damawand.de)](https://www.damawand.de/Ski/Abali.html), [Abali Ski Resort (ITTO)](https://itto.org/iran/attraction/abali-ski-resort-damavand-haraaz-road), [Abali ski history (Kojaro, Persian)](https://www.kojaro.com/travel-photos/307673-photo-abali-ski-history/).

> ### Samsun and the tobacco trade
>
> - **Why Samsun**: Samsun was long one of the main tobacco centres of Turkey. Tobacco was a major source of Ottoman revenue, and the French-run Régie opened a tobacco factory in Samsun in 1887. The railway link and the factory helped the trade boom, and farming in Samsun's hinterland shifted from grain to tobacco. Industry growth continued into the early 1980s.
> - **State monopoly**: In the 20th century the state company Tekel became the sole manufacturer and distributor of tobacco and alcohol in Turkey. In a later study of farms around Samsun, about half of the tobacco was bought by Tekel and half by merchants, which gives an idea of how the buying was split. This is not specific to 1961.
> - **Fit with the slides**: Slides 30 and 31 show men in flat caps inspecting wrapped bales of leaf tobacco. This fits a tobacco buying market of the kind found around Samsun, but nothing in the images or on the box says the market was in Samsun.
> - **Not found**: I did not find anything specific about the 1960s bale-auction system.
> - **Sources**: [Tobacco production in Samsun, 1788–1919](https://www.sosyalarastirmalar.com/articles/tobacco-production-in-samsun-17881919.pdf), [Samsun (Black Sea cities)](https://cities.blacksea.gr/en/samsun/4-1-1), [Tekel (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tekel), [Tobacco breeding and the tobacco factory in Samsun](https://avesis.omu.edu.tr/yayin/e2a634a8-81f3-49c1-aa86-d68a4a642ef2/examining-tobacco-breeding-and-the-tobacco-factory-in-samsun-through-written-and-oral-sources).

> ### Le barrage de Karaj (barrage Amir Kabir)
>
> - **Dates** : construit de 1957 à 1961 (1336 à 1340 du calendrier iranien) et mis en service en 1961. C'est décrit comme le premier barrage à usages multiples d'Iran, un barrage-voûte en béton qui fournit l'eau potable de Téhéran et l'irrigation. Les premières études remontent aux années 1930 et 1940. Une source persane donne une période un peu plus tardive, d'environ 1958 à 1963.
> - **Constructeur** : les sources divergent : l'entreprise américaine Morrison-Knudsen (1957 à 1961) pour l'une, une entreprise française pour une autre. Ce point n'est pas tranché.

> ### Samsun et le commerce du tabac
>
> - **Pourquoi Samsun** : Samsun fut longtemps l'un des principaux centres du tabac en Turquie. Le tabac était une grande source de revenus de l'Empire ottoman, et la Régie, d'administration française, a ouvert une manufacture de tabac à Samsun en 1887. Le chemin de fer et la manufacture ont fait prospérer le commerce, et l'arrière-pays de Samsun est passé des céréales au tabac. La croissance de l'industrie a continué jusqu'au début des années 1980.
> - **Monopole d'État** : au XXe siècle, l'entreprise publique Tekel est devenue le seul fabricant et distributeur de tabac et d'alcool en Turquie. Dans une étude plus récente sur des exploitations autour de Samsun, environ la moitié du tabac était achetée par Tekel et la moitié par des négociants, ce qui donne une idée de la répartition des achats. Cela ne concerne pas précisément 1961.
> - **Cohérence avec les diapositives** : les diapositives 30 et 31 montrent des hommes en casquette qui examinent des balles de tabac en feuilles emballées. Cela correspond à un marché d'achat de tabac comme ceux de la région de Samsun, mais rien dans les images ni sur la boîte ne dit que ce marché était à Samsun.
> - **Non trouvé** : je n'ai rien trouvé de précis sur le système de vente aux enchères des balles dans les années 1960.
> - **Sources** : [Production de tabac à Samsun, 1788–1919](https://www.sosyalarastirmalar.com/articles/tobacco-production-in-samsun-17881919.pdf), [Samsun (villes de la mer Noire)](https://cities.blacksea.gr/en/samsun/4-1-1), [Tekel (Wikipédia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tekel), [Sélection du tabac et manufacture de Samsun](https://avesis.omu.edu.tr/yayin/e2a634a8-81f3-49c1-aa86-d68a4a642ef2/examining-tobacco-breeding-and-the-tobacco-factory-in-samsun-through-written-and-oral-sources).
