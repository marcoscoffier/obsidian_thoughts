> ### Notes
>
> - "Recht" is the old French spelling of Rasht, the capital of Gilan province on the Caspian coast. The folder name says April 1959.
> - The Super 8 film 2972072-04 (box: "C5 – Voyage Sefid Rud – Racht – Mer Caspienne") shows the same events: the crowded town square, the open-air kitchen with cauldrons, the clerics around a telephone, a brick building under construction, net fishing on the Caspian. Film C4 is labelled "fin février 1959", so C5 and these slides date most likely from spring 1959.
> - The ceremony (schoolgirls carrying a leafy garland, officials, the Shah's portrait with the flag, a cleric with a telephone among metal cabinets) looks like the opening of a telephone exchange or a similar public work. That is a hypothesis from the cabinets; no caption confirms it.
> - Slide 22 is `raw_test.jpg`, a second scan of slide 14 with different colour grading, probably a scanning test.
