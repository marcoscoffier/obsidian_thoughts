> ### Notes
>
> - The box gives three parts: Damavand, village and camp, summit. Slides 1–4 are views of the cone from the valley; 5–10 are a village with a bazaar lane (probably Reineh, where the ascent starts; compare the 1959 Super 8 box "Reneh – Barzah Sevum – Sommet") and, in slide 7, bathing in a mountain river; 11–27 climb to the high camp; 28–37 are the summit zone.
> - Near the top the rocks are stained yellow by sulphur from the fumaroles, and the snow forms "penitentes" (ice spikes), as in the 1959 film of the same mountain (Super 8 reel 2972072-09, "Demavend avec Frejaques et Lana, Été 1959").
> - The party of the high camp (two men, one in blue, one in a light jacket) and the man in the blue jacket at the tent are not named.
