# Sky Color

> [!note] About this file
> Printed directions for viewers, set twice on the page (to cut in half). On view 29 March – 5 April, 5:30–7pm. The year comes from the French label.

--Sky Color--
Will be on view
Wed. March 29- Wed. Apr. 5 from 5:30pm- 7:00pm

From campus:
Walk up Prospect to Sachem St.
Cross Sachem.
Osborn Labs is on the right hand side of Prospect--opposite Ingalls Rink.
The building cuts the corner at a diagonal.
There is a large gateway in the middle of the building through which
you can see the lights to a green house.
The sun is setting behind you.
Please procede through the gateway.

From Hammond Hall:
Walk up Sachem to the corner of Prospect where you can see the Osborn
Lab gateway. Then as above.
