# Fleeting Purple — Sketch for Senior Project

Fleeting Purple--sketch for Senior Project

Feb. 15, 1995.

Materials:
--Shadow from strong sunlight on the snow
--Orange mesh site-fence partly in the full sunlight, partly in the
shade through which the shadow can be seen.

Directions to site (frame):

Starting on the corner of Hillhouse and Trumbull walk up Hillhouse towards Kline Biology Tower and Science Hill, following the left hand sidewalk. The fence in question is the second orange mesh fence you will see. It is much longer than the first and leads down a walkway on the left side of the new Henry Luce building. Optimum viewing distance is about twenty feet away from the fence while still on the Hillhouse sidewalk, there will be a high chain link fence immediately on your left. The left hand portion of the fence is in the shade of the building on your left, the right hand side of the fence is in full sunlight. Notice how the shadow on the snow through the part of the fence which is in the shade looks to be its normal blue, whereas the shadow seen through the fence in full sunlight looks purple.

The piece is on view any sunny middle to late afternoon when there is snow on the ground and as long as the fence remains in place.
