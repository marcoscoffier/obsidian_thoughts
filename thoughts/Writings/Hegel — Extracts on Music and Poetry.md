# Hegel — Extracts on Music and Poetry

...music must, on account of its one sidedness, call on the help of the more exact meaning of words and, in order to become more firmly conjoined with the detail and characteristic expression of the subject matter, it demands a text which alone gives a fuller content to the subjective's life outpouring in the notes.(p.388)

Poetry has as its general principle spirituality ...it expresses directly for its own apprehension the spirit with all its imaginative and artistic conceptions but without setting these out visibly and bodily for contemplation from the outside.(p.388)

..Poetry is exempt from painting's restriction to a specific space and still more to one specific feature of a situation or action, it is given the possibility of presenting a subject in its whole inward depth and in the breadth of its temporal development.

A movie strengthen's the other two artforms with respect to poetry. Music can have meaning without having to rely on words to carry it, and images are no longer restricted to one space

Now, let us examine how he justifies poetry as the mediation between music and painting. He says:

Painting... has an overall advantage when it is a matter of bringing a subject before our eyes in its external appearance...poetry is incapable of reaching the definiteness of sense perception.(p.389) Hegel constantly seems to say that spirit is words.

This quote expressly describes a distance between what is being communicated and spirit or words. This raises a problem I have with the placement of the spirit. I had been under the impression that it was some thing internal in man which searched for new forms in which to express itself, but if it becomes only what is communicated this becomes something else all together. :

To say that the artform representative of 1994, is not poetry might seem to require a complete upheaval of the Hegelian system, but I am not so much replacing poetry with a new art form; I am thinking of film as poetry with images and music.

I propose that the form of art which encapsulates the progress of Mind/Spirit's progress toward greater self-consciousness in 1994 is one which was not around in Hegel's time--film. s to choose film as the art form for 1994 requires a change in our concept of geist.. But Hegel has proscribed an end to this movement, and we have moved one hundred and sixty-five years beyond that end. There are no new artforms, photography is a techincal advancement in the artform of making images which was the domain of painting. Film, or moving pictures, could be looked at as a new artform, but I see film, and I'm thinking of it in 1994, as a combination of many artforms which were known to Hegel--theatre, painting, poetry, and music. Hegel has already assigned the major artforms to different time periods: architecture to the symbolic, sculpture to the classical, painting and music to the romantic, and poetry to the modern, so it seems plausible that if we wish to move beyond Hegel, and find a text which is representative of 1994, it should be one which is a combination of many artforms. I wish to argue that the text which can best express geist in 1994 is not mearly poetic but rather combines Hegel's three romantic art forms--poetry, art, and music
