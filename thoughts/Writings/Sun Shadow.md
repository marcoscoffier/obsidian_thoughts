# Sun Shadow

> [!note] About this file
> Directions for viewers, on view 1–8 May, 6:30–8:30pm; printed twice on the page.

Sun Shadow
Will be on view every evening (weather permitting)
from 6:30-8:30
May 1- May 8
To get to the installation:

Procede up Prospect street to the top of science hill, which you will find is a favorite path for many student joggers. The right hand side walk is lined by an elbow-high stone wall which ends a few hundred feet from the top of the hill. A square green sign on a light post advertizing "Science Park" with an arrow tells you that you have arrived at Edwards Street. The hill flattens out considerably and on the other side of the street you see some scraggly bushes with yellow flowers intersperced with short long-needled evergreens. This corner often smells very strongly of bark.

At the first break in a low wooden fence which encloses these bushes, you see four steps which lead to a path between two much taller evergreens with redish trunks. A faded blue sign on an awkward rusted post tells you that you are entering "The William Whitman Farnum Memorial Garden given in 1931 by his widow etc." You can see many wonderfully particular trees. Each seems to have its own personality which is dampened only by the stubs of branches which have been cut because they lay too close to the ground.

As you procede up the asphalt path, it takes a turn back to the right past a tree whose lower trunk is particularly pained by wart-like protrusions which resemble sagging flesh. On your left are two trees whose downwards falling tortured branches seem awfully haggish. The sun is setting almost directly behind you. You can follow the path further or cut across the grass on your way to the stand made of rather blond wood which is the final stop on your trip.
