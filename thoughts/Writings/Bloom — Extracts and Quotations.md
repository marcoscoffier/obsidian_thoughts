Let us quickly compare this to a passage from Ammons' "Corsons Inlet:

a young mottled gull stood free on the shoals and ate
to vomiting: another gull, squawking possession, cracked a crab, picked out the entrails, swallowed the soft-shelled legs, a ruddy turnstone running in to snatch leftover bits:

The image of this gull pecking the life out of its shell, is a reversal of Ashbery's where the life is breaking out of its shell ready to violate the frightened plain. And here an animal in nature is pecking out the life, not man. Nature is terrible before man ever came on the scene.
