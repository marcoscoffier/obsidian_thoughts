# Poem with Rhythms (Wallace Stevens)

> [!note] About this file
> Copied in two columns that ran together when the file was saved as text; left as found.

The hand between the
Grows large on the wall.

The mind between this
. . .
Grows large against space.
candle and the wall
light or that and space,
It must be that the hand
Has a will to grow larger
To grow larger and heavier
The wall; and that the
Turns to its own
on the wall,
and stronger than mind
figurations and declares,
"This image, this love, I
Of these. In these, I come
In these, I wear a vital
Not as in air, bright-blue-

compose myself
forth outwardly.

cleanliness,

resembling air,

But as in the powerful mirror

--Wallace Stevens

of my wish and will."
