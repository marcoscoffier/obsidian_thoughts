# Experiencing space

How space, depth and distance are lived rather than measured: the senior essay on Merleau-Ponty, Bachelard, Camus and Saint-Exupéry, its notes and fragments, and the art texts that come out of the same question.

*Left out:* Pictorial space in paintings (Hopper, Diebenkorn) is left out: that is space on a canvas, not space experienced.

13 texts. A text is in this theme when its `themes` property links here; add or remove the link to change that.

```base
filters:
  and:
    - file.inFolder("Writings")
    - file.hasLink(this.file)
views:
  - type: table
    name: Writings
    order:
      - file.name
      - year
      - type
      - course
      - words
    sort:
      - property: year
        direction: ASC
```
