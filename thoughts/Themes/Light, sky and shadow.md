# Light, sky and shadow

Sunlight, sky colour, twilight and shadow as subject matter: the 1995 installations, the quotations gathered for them, the letter on atmospheric refraction, the sunlit bathroom of the sculpture notes.

8 texts. A text is in this theme when its `themes` property links here; add or remove the link to change that.

filters:
  and:
    - file.inFolder("Writings")

    name: Writings
    order:
      - file.name
      - year
      - type
      - course
      - words
